# 1. Structural Fallacies in Current Fiat & MMT Interpretations

## 1.1 The core problem: Fiat is not understood globally
Modern fiat systems represent *sovereign credit operations with tax-backed value anchoring*, but this fact is almost entirely unrecognized in economic practice.

### Key failures:
- Textbook monetary theory preserved pre-1971 gold-standard assumptions.
- Policymakers interpret government finance using household analogies.
- International institutions lack a unified transparency protocol.

## 1.2 MMT: Correct description, incorrect policy
### Accurate (descriptive) components:
- Taxes function as value stabilizers, not funding.
- Bond issuance is an asset swap.
- Currency-issuing governments cannot involuntarily default.

### Incorrect (policy) components:
- Over-reliance on "inflation constraint" derived from a closed-economy model.
- Lack of cross-border capital flow considerations.
- Absence of a mechanism to prevent political misuse.

## 1.3 Why this matters for GSSH
Misinterpretations of fiat operations exacerbate:
- Systemic stagnation
- Policy divergence
- Capital misallocation
- Social instability

Fiat-U addresses these failures as an interpretive transparency OS.
