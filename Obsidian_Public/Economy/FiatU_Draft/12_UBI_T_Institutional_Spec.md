# UBI-T Institutional Specification (Draft)

> **Note:** This file defines the institutional design of UBI-T as the *implementation layer* of Fiat-U. It intentionally avoids distribution formulas or benefit amounts, to prevent misuse and preserve policy neutrality.

## 1. Scope and Intent
UBI-T is not a welfare program. It is a *transparency-indexed income stabilizer* anchored in Fiat-U.

- Financed by **transparency-adjusted seigniorage (T-seigniorage)** rather than taxes.
- Distributed automatically via CLEAR-ID.
- Designed to *absorb systemic volatility* revealed by GSSH.

## 2. Eligibility
- Universality principle: all residents with a valid CLEAR-ID key.
- Exclusions: none; fraud control is handled by CLEAR-OPS, not by eligibility screening.

## 3. Funding Mechanism
- Annual envelope is tied to the **T-value band**.
- Adjusts automatically: no political discretion.
- Monetary stability constraint is governed by the Fiat-U council.

## 4. Anti-Fraud Mechanism (Non-Technical)
To avoid revealing technical details:
- Identity uniqueness ensured by "single-route attestation".
- Temporal consistency checks on benefit claims.
- Statistical anomaly detection performed without personal inspection.

## 5. Reporting Protocol
- Annual public report to IMF Article IV.
- Quarterly dashboard (non-personal) for OECD.

## 6. CLEAR Integration (Institutional only)
- CLEAR provides timestamp authority.
- No behavioral tracking is used.
- Nodes may not run inference or analytics beyond eligibility proof.

## 7. Transition Path
- Phase 0 (Pilot): Japan.
- Phase 1: OECD opt-in.
- Phase 2: IMF extended framework.
