# 3. Connecting Fiat-U to GSSH

Fiat-U solves the *invisibility* problem that GSSH identifies as the upstream driver of global stagnation.

## 3.1 GSSH → Fiat-U logical flow
- Systemic stagnation arises from misaligned models
- Misalignment arises from institutional opacity
- Opacity arises because fiat systems lack a global standard
- Fiat-U provides the missing standard

## 3.2 Expected macro effects
- Reduction of mispricing in sovereign debt
- Stabilization of global capital allocation
- Enhanced policy credibility
- Prevention of crisis escalation

## 3.3 Practical deployment path
- T-value pilot in Japan
- Formal endorsement by a regional coalition (G7/IMF-compatible)
- Gradual expansion to transparency-seeking states
