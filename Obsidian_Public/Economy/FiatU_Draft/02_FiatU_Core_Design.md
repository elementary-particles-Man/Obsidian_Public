# 2. Fiat-U Core Design (Draft)

## 2.1 Nature of Fiat-U
Fiat-U is **not**:
- A new currency
- A supra-sovereign monetary authority
- A replacement for central banks

Fiat-U *is*:
- A transparency overlay
- A standardized interpretive protocol
- A method for evaluating sovereign fiscal space
- A stabilizer that prevents MMT-type misuse

## 2.2 The Transparency Index (T-value)
A cross-sovereign benchmark measuring:
- Fiscal data accuracy
- Bond market structure resilience
- Tax system clarity
- Central bank–treasury coordination
- Institutional auditability

## 2.3 Integration with existing institutions
- IMF Article IV consultations
- BIS–FSB data structures
- G20 fiscal transparency codes

Fiat-U adds interpretability, not authority.
