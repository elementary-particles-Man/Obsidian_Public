# 4. UBI-T as an Application Layer

## 4.1 Concept
UBI-T is not a welfare scheme but a *distribution protocol* backed by:
- CLEAR architecture
- Fiat-U transparency
- Verifiable fiscal space

## 4.2 Role in resolving GSSH stagnation
- Stabilizes demand without inflationary bias
- Reduces political extremism
- Supports long-run productivity

## 4.3 Relationship with Fiat-U
Fiat-U: explains what is possible
UBI-T: implements what is desirable

The two form a complete interpretive + operational stack.
