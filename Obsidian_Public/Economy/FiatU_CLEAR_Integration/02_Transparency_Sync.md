# Transparency Sync Protocol (Institutional Description)

## Purpose
Provide countries a *neutral mechanism* for reporting transparency indicators.

## Data Types
- Institutional: budgets, timetables, audits
- Structural: investment cycles, governance delays
- NOT personal

## Transmission
- Once per quarter
- CLEAR ensures timestamp integrity only

## Boundaries
- Cannot access national datasets
- Cannot enforce policy
- Acts solely as *integrity oracle*
