# Preconditions for Ultimate

## 1. Institutional
- Fiat-U operating for > 20–30 years
- Global T-value stabilization
- Collapse of incentive structures based on opacity

## 2. Technological
- CLEAR nodes standardized internationally
- Universal timestamp availability
- Non-behavioral identity proofs

## 3. Social
- Decline of zero-sum political competition
- Rise of transparency-based legitimacy

## 4. Economic
- Reduced dependence on credit engines
- Shift toward structural output metrics
