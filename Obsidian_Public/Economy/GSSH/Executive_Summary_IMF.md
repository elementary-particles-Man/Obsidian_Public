# Executive Summary — GSSH (IMF Format)

**Draft — Not for Citation**

The Global Systemic Stagnation Hypothesis (GSSH) argues that the world economy is being constrained by four reinforcing macrostructural forces: monetary exhaustion, demographic decline, asymmetric technological productivity, and rising geopolitical fragmentation.

These forces interact in a systemic manner, producing a stagnation loop that conventional macro models cannot fully capture.

The hypothesis is preliminary and intended as a conceptual framework for further research.
