# ============================================

# 📘 **RCGF Charter: English Draft**

# ============================================

## Formal Name and Abbreviations (External / Internal)

### External Formal Name (Final)

**Framework for Reality-Based Cooperative Governance (RCGF)**

- No numbers or fixed membership implied
- Defined as a cooperative layer, not an organization or bloc

### Internal / Historical Name (Origin Retention)

**E7-RCGF**

- "E7" is retained solely as an origin code for design history and accountability
- Not to be used in outward-facing documents, statements, or titles

---

## Preamble

```md
This Framework, originating from the E7 initiative,
is hereinafter referred to as the
“Framework for Reality-Based Cooperative Governance (RCGF)”.

RCGF is not a fixed-membership organization, alliance, or bloc,
but a cooperative governance framework designed to operate
under conditions where conventional institutional,
financial, and political assumptions no longer hold.
```

This Charter is conceived by civilians and is not an official statement of any state,
government, or international organization. It does not seek interference, harm,
or pressure on public entities. Its purpose is to define a reality-based cooperative layer.

RCGF operates on the principle that verifiable records and accountability mechanisms
are necessary for trust, while the specific technical implementations remain
outside the scope of this Framework.

---

# ===============================
# Article 0: Operational Stance
# ===============================

## **Article 0: Principle of Non-Catastrophic Coordination**

RCGF does not pursue optimal outcomes based on ideological, historical,
or moral premises. It exists solely to enable survivable, non-catastrophic
coordination under conditions where no universally “correct” solution exists.

---

# ===============================
# Article 1: Reality First
# ===============================

## **Article 1: Principle of Reality Supremacy**

All ideals fall before reality. We therefore aim to perceive reality accurately
and improve it correctly, to protect every life within our scope, share prosperity,
and contribute to peaceful order by continually capturing a changing reality.

---

# ===============================
# Article 2: Non-Justice and the Three Crowns
# ===============================

## **Article 2: Principle of Non-Justice and the Three Crowns**

**We renounce the conceit that we are always right.  
No actor, including ourselves, possesses eternal justice.  
Therefore, we do not place “justice” as our supreme crown.**

**Instead, we uphold the following three as enduring norms:**

1. **Transparency**  
   Procedures are visible; no power is exercised under opacity.

2. **Fairness**  
   Arbitrary rules are rejected; standards apply equally to all.

3. **Accuracy**  
   Procedures remain reproducible; outcomes do not change by who intervenes.

**These three crowns are the highest principles of RCGF governance.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 3: Inviolability of Life and Minimum Dignity
# ===============================

1. ## **Article 3: Principle of the Inviolability of Life and the Minimum Dignity Guarantee**

**We recognize an equal right to live for all people.  
No life may be denied its existence.**

**Even in extreme poverty, every person retains the right to live with a minimum of dignity,  
and we guarantee this.**

**“Human” here is not defined solely by physical form but by the presence of humanity worth protecting,  
the capacity to value others’ lives and dignity.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 4: Evidence, Transparency, and Non-Coercion
# ===============================

1.## **Article 4: Principle of Evidence Integrity and Non-Coercive Transparency**

**To uphold the three crowns (transparency, fairness, accuracy), RCGF bears the highest duty.**

**This is achieved through tamper-resistant records, rigorously maintained, ensuring that
verifiable audit trails exist at all times.**

**These audit trails exist to preserve order and stewardship; we reject their use as tools
that bind or suppress freedom.**

**No actor may wield audit trails as a means of oppression.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 5: Trust
# ===============================

## **Article 5: Principle of Trust**

We define trust as the most critical indicator of governance.  
It is difficult to build and lost in an instant.

We act with this asymmetry in mind and govern with integrity so as not to damage trust.
Governance ensures that all people can maintain healthy, cultural lives without damage to honor or dignity; we eliminate anything contrary.

---

# ===============================
# Article 6: Cooperative Model
# ===============================

1. RCGF adopts a **lightweight cooperative layer** distinct from conventional political coordination or integrationism.  
2. Central authority is minimized; the sovereignty and autonomy of each participating entity are maximized.  
3. Cooperation is limited to the domains of economy, currency, authentication, and transparency.

---

# ===============================
# Article 7: Essential Infrastructure and Collective Defense
# ===============================

## **Article 7: Principle of Essential Infrastructure Inviolability and Collective Defense**

**Life is the most precious asset.  
Therefore, we pledge to defend—through concerted action—the public infrastructures essential to life:  
water, food, energy, healthcare, and communications.**

**If these infrastructures are attacked or destroyed by any actor, RCGF will exercise collective self-defense and impose necessary reparations and obligations for restoration on that actor.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 8: Allocation of Responsibilities
# ===============================

1. **To fulfill Article 7, participating entities allocate responsibilities according to capability.**

- Define domains such as regional coordination, defense, food supply, energy supply, technology, administrative efficiency, and financial asset stewardship; assign them through open agreement.
- Review assignments as conditions change; no role is permanently fixed to any actor.
- Keep and publish minutes of role assignments and revisions to ensure verifiability.

---

# ===============================
# Article 9: Concord, Trust, and Cooperative Engagement
# ===============================

1.## **Article 9: Principle of Concord, Trust, and Cooperative Engagement**

**Our operating rule is simple: we begin external negotiations with concord.**

**While relationships continue, we pursue sincere, mutually beneficial cooperation.**

**If trust is damaged, we maintain an adversarial stance toward the actor—excluding humanitarian aid—until trust is fully restored.**

**This principle applies equally to ourselves and collaborators.  
Any actor wishing to join must fully agree to this Charter;  
its eligibility is deliberated in a public forum and decided openly.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 10: Reconciling Ideals and Reality; Restoring Fellowship
# ===============================

## **Article 10: Principle of Reconciliation Between Reality and Ideals**

**We do not flee from reality.  
These articles are ideals, and forcing ideals can bring misfortune depending on circumstances.**

**As Article 1 states, ideals yield to reality.  
When colleagues face hostile conditions, we rely on openness and non-violence to repair ties.**

**If all measures fail, we part ways with that actor based on a fully public consensus.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 11: Common Currency and Absolute Trust Value
# ===============================

1. ## **Article 11: Principle of the Common Currency and Absolute Trust Value**

**RCGF is a cooperation of entities that each hold currency issuance rights;  
any adoption of a common currency requires agreement of all participants.**

**The common currency is defined to carry an “absolute trust value” and may be exchanged with each currency by relative valuation.**
The “absolute trust value” is defined as non-speculative and non-derivative in nature.

**All transactions and exchanges are strictly preserved as audit trails and cannot be tampered with.**

**Detailed management rules are set by the entity charged with credit stewardship;  
all participants bear obligations for acceptance and exchange into their own currencies.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 12: Two-layer Administration
# ===============================

1. ## **Article 12: Principle of Dual-Layered Executive Authority**

**Executive authority within RCGF has two layers:  
① domestic executive authority held by each entity;  
② common executive authority for RCGF-wide interests, security, and infrastructure.**

**The common authority has direct effect only within the scope defined by the Charter.**

**Domestic authority is exercised independently under each entity’s constitution and laws.**

# ===============================
# Article 14: Judicial Authority
# ===============================

1.## **Article 14: Principle of Limited Supranational Judicial Authority**

**Judicial authority within RCGF has two layers:**

1. **RCGF Court**  
   Holds superior jurisdiction only for Charter violations, major cases related to the common currency or trust value, attacks on shared infrastructure, and violations of audit-trail preservation.

2. **Domestic Courts**  
   Criminal, civil, and administrative matters not directly related to the Charter remain fully under domestic jurisdiction.

**Supranational jurisdiction stays minimal, and domestic independence is respected to the maximum.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 16: Taxation and Fiscal Adjustment
# ===============================

1. ## **Article 16: Principle of Fiscal Authority and Taxation**

**Under this Charter, RCGF may establish a common tax regime, limited to matters directly tied to shared infrastructure, security, and the trust value of the common currency.**

**Establishment or revision of the common tax system requires agreement of all participants; domestic tax systems remain sovereign.**

**Fiscal adjustments rely on the common currency and verifiable audit trails; the three crowns of transparency, fairness, and accuracy apply strictly.**

---

# ===============================
# Article 17: Internal Security and Cooperative Investigations
# ===============================

## **Article 17: Principle of Internal Security and Cooperative Investigations**

**1. For major crimes, cross-border offenses, attacks on infrastructure, or Charter-related violations within RCGF, participants must employ multi-entity cooperative investigations rather than single-actor or bilateral-only approaches.**

*Bilateral settings risk political pressure; overly large groups hinder execution. RCGF defaults to balanced multi-actor cooperation.*

---

**2. Cooperative investigations require:**

- Full connectivity with relevant public international bodies
- Tamper-resistant audit-trail preservation
- Mutual verification of investigative data
- Exclusion of arbitrary political intervention
- Non-disclosure obligations during investigations

---

**3. Investigative data may remain confidential for as long as reasonably required after case closure.  
Extensions need a special resolution agreed by the RCGF judicial body.**
