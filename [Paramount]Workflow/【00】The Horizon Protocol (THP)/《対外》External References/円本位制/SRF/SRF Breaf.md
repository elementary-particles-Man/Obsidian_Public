# ✅ **Executive Brief（1-Page Version）

for Bank of Japan / G7 / PM Office**

**Title: Strategic Reconstruction Framework (SRF)  
and Post-CCP Recovery Plan for Mainland China**

---

## **1. Purpose**

This brief outlines:

1. **SRF – Semiconductor Reconstruction Fund**  
    A rapid, globally coordinated program to replace China-dependent supply chains.
    
2. **Post-CCP China Recovery Plan (THP-0 China)**  
    A _non-intervention_, _humanitarian-first_, _city-unit_ reconstruction framework.
    

Both documents avoid political value-judgment and focus strictly on global stability.

---

## **2. Background: Systemic Collapse of China**

- China’s industrial capacity is entering **structural failure** (power grid collapse, water scarcity, halted chemical plants, semiconductor dead-lines).
    
- Supply chains cannot recover within any meaningful time horizon.
    
- World markets face a synchronized shock in **batteries, chemicals, PCBs, DRAM/NAND**.
    

---

## **3. SRF – Semiconductor Reconstruction Fund**

### **Objective**

Replace China-dependent production with a diversified G7–ASEAN supply network within **18–36 months**.

### **Core Principles**

- **Not a bailout** – a **global substitution program**.
    
- **Selective rescue only**（Hynix is viable; Samsung = partial & limited to critical lines）
    
- **Japan-led financing**, **U.S. technological anchoring**, **ASEAN deployment**.
    

### **Key Actions**

1. **Hynix stabilization package** (JPYC-backed liquidity + restructuring).
    
2. **Kioxia–Micron anchor alliance** (DRAM/NAND redundancy).
    
3. **Japan chemical & battery surge capacity** (rapid scaling via national program).
    
4. **ASEAN relocation for PCBs, assembly, and packaging**.
    
5. **CLEAR-based audit trail** for transparency and international trust.
    

### **Budget**

Approx. **¥10 trillion** (≈ USD 65–70bn).  
_“Cheap for preventing global semiconductor failure.”_

---

## **4. THP-0 China (Post-CCP Reconstruction)**

### **Principles**

- **No regime-change operations**
    
- **Strict non-intervention**
    
- **Humanitarian corridors first**
    
- **City-by-city administrative restoration**
    
- **Infrastructure recovery without political alignment**
    

### **Why City Units?**

Nation-wide governance restoration is impossible;  
Shanghai, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Chongqing, Tianjin, etc.  
must be treated as individual humanitarian+economic zones.

### **Japan’s Role**

- Provide **logistics & verification**
    
- Deploy **CLEAR transparency layer**
    
- No military presence
    
- No political leverage exercised
    

---

## **5. Strategic Outcome**

- Rapid stabilization of global supply chains
    
- Controlled transition in mainland China
    
- Dollar-dependence reduced without destabilizing markets
    
- Yen-anchored “stability zone” emerging naturally
    
- G7 unity preserved even if U.S. policy is unstable
