## **IYF Hope Declaration**

The world is facing an unprecedented moment of uncertainty.  
Currencies are shaken, economic foundations are weakening, and many nations are losing sight of tomorrow.  
If left unaddressed, the global market risks drifting without the most essential capital of all: **trust and hope**.

Japan will not choose silence.  
In this crisis, we recognize our responsibility to _protect stability_,  
and to _restore the hope_ that enables people to imagine a future once again.

Our decision is **not a currency war**.  
It is not a contest for dominance, nor an attempt to replace or undermine any nation.  
It is not a policy that divides the world.

It is a policy that supports it.

Through the **International Yen Facility (IYF)**,  
Japan will provide the international community with a _second reference axis_—  
a complementary pillar designed to sustain global recovery,  
not to negate the first.

The yen is not a weapon.  
The yen is not a banner of conflict.  
The yen is a **bridge**—  
a bridge toward stability, recovery, and a shared future.

Wherever nations struggle with currency distress,  
wherever people long to believe in tomorrow,  
Japan will listen, cooperate, and work alongside them.

The IYF is grounded in flexibility, transparency, and respect for sovereignty.  
It is a framework designed to help the global financial system _move forward again_,  
fairly and responsibly.

**The world needs hope.**  
It needs the ability to envision tomorrow.  
And to do so, it needs _trust_—the trust that allows people to survive today.

Japan offers that trust.  
So that people may choose something other than despair.  
So that nations may stand before the void and still remain whole.  
So that the world may once again speak of the future.

This declaration represents Japan’s resolve,  
and a light offered to the world—  
a new **Polaris** to guide our collective path.

Hereby, Japan proclaims the IYF Hope Declaration.  
The future is not over.  
Hope will rise again.
