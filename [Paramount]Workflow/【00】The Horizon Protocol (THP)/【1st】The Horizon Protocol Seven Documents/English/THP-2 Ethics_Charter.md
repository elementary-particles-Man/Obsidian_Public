### **THP-2: Ethics Charter**

### **Preamble**

**Academic Version**

This Charter defines the ethical foundation of the new world order established by The Horizon Protocol (THP). It directly confronts the reality that conventional international law and theories of justice have become dysfunctional, and establishes the **"maintenance of order" and "defense of human morality"** as absolute standards. All nations, organizations, and individuals will be evaluated based on the articles of this Charter, and the rightness of their actions will be judged accordingly.

**Explanatory Version (Explanation of Order in THP)**

In an ideal world, rules and charters would be unnecessary.

However, reality, like natural selection and the law of the jungle, mercilessly brings both happiness and disaster.

For humans to live with dignity as "human beings," a wall is necessary to protect themselves from this harsh reality.

That wall is **"human morality," and within it, we survive through "collective self-defense."**

Any act that destroys this wall becomes a "sin." There are two types of sin: **"atonement,"** which allows for reintegration into the community after repentance and reparation, and **"condemnation,"** which is never forgiven and results in permanent expulsion beyond the wall.

Order in THP is nothing other than the unwavering defense of this collective self-defense, with "human morality" as the sole criterion and through the strict rules of "atonement and condemnation."

---

### **Article X: Reality Precedence Clause - Provisions Regarding Discrepancies Between Human Morality and Reality**

Human morality is a covenant with reality that must never be broken for humans to live as human beings.

Its details are described in THP-5 "Lexicon," but specifically, it inherits the principles of "crimes against humanity in the old order (Geneva Conventions, Rome Statute, etc.)," and its redefinition, including additions and amendments, will be democratically determined by the "Global Council of Ethics" described later.

**THP has neither the authority nor the qualification to unilaterally define human morality.**

**Academic Version**

1.  When theory and reality are in conflict, **reality is always correct**, and theory is merely a tool to explain reality.
2.  Even when the ideals of human morality conflict with the constraints of reality, we must **accept the constraints of reality** and pursue the realization of human morality on that basis.
3.  However, the ultimate purpose of the world order, including human morality, lies in the **affirmation of the sustainable existence** of humanity and all of creation.
4.  Therefore, in all rulings, including condemnation, the judging party must respect the ideals of human morality to the greatest extent possible, while also directly confronting the incompatible constraints of reality and carrying out enforcement that is as consistent with human morality as possible under those constraints.

**Explanatory Version**

-   If "idealism" and "harsh reality" clash, we always prioritize reality. Theory is a tool to overcome reality.
-   Even a high ideal like human morality must first accept reality if it is practically impossible to implement.
-   However, the true purpose of human morality and world rules is to "enable both people and nature to continue to exist."
-   Therefore, even when punishment is given, it is done in a way that is as consistent with human morality as possible within the scope of reality, without forgetting the ideal.

### **Ethical Institutions (Names and Hierarchy)**

#### **Global Council of Ethics**

**Academic Version**

As the highest decision-making body, it has the sole authority to declare "condemnation" and "atonement" in the name of all humanity. Its jurisdiction is limited to universal high crimes, and its decisions are recorded as irreversible historical stigmas.

**Explanatory Version**

The **"final judge"** of all humanity.

It is the only place that ultimately decides whether to "forgive (atonement)" or "never forgive (condemnation)."

#### **National Councils of Ethics**

**Academic Version**

Established in each integrated entity (nations, governments, supranational organizations, etc.). They are responsible for fact-finding, evidence gathering, initial hearings, and sentencing recommendations, and report their conclusions to the Global Council of Ethics. However, they do not have final sentencing authority.

**Explanatory Version**

The **"investigation and prosecution teams"** of each country and organization.

When an incident occurs, they investigate it thoroughly and report to the Global Council of Ethics, "This is the appropriate punishment."

#### **Humanity Study Group**

**Academic Version**

An advisory body composed of the Four Sages (tentatively designated as His Majesty the Emperor of Japan, the Pope, the King of Saudi Arabia, and King Richard). As an independent body with no hierarchical superiority over other ethics councils, it exercises only two specific powers: the **"right to request pardon" and the "right to reconsider condemnation."**

**Explanatory Version**

The "conscience of humanity."

It has a special role to play, such as calling for a halt when a punishment is too severe, or suggesting, "Perhaps we should forgive them now," for those who have deeply repented.

It has only these two powers and is not superior to the others.

### **[Special Note] Regarding the Selection of the Humanity Study Group (Four Sages)**

**Academic Version**

The current composition of the Four Sages is provisional. Its essence lies in its function as a **"safety device to introduce human emotion and wisdom into cold logic."** The current composition is a symbolic authority representing major cultural spheres, but it is clear that it lacks universality in terms of secular/non-religious perspectives, geographical/cultural balance, and gender balance. The official composition shall be reconsidered through a global democratic process after the new order has stabilized.

**Explanatory Version**

The members of the Four Sages are, for the time being, placeholders.

The true role of this organization is as a safety device to reflect human emotions, conscience, and wisdom cultivated over a long history, in order to prevent a "cold system of logic alone" from running rampant.

The current members are biased towards religion and royalty, and there are no representatives from science, women, or regions such as Africa and South America.

Originally, a more diverse representation should be re-elected democratically, but as we are currently in a state of emergency, crisis response is prioritized, and this provisional composition is in place.

### **Article 0: The Nature and Granting of Human Rights**

**Academic Version**

Human rights are not inherent, but are qualifications granted only to members of a community who abide by human morality. The granting body has a two-tiered structure, established through both (1) domestic granting by the group to which each individual belongs, and (2) international granting based on a global consensus. Therefore, human rights are guaranteed as part of collective self-defense, conditional on the maintenance of order.

**Explanatory Version**

Human rights are not something that are "naturally possessed from birth."

Only those who follow the common rule of "human morality" are granted human rights as members of their country or group.

Furthermore, "international human rights" are also guaranteed by the consensus of all humanity.

In other words, human rights are a qualification for maintaining the community, conditional on "maintaining order."

(Naturally, for those who cannot fulfill their responsibilities themselves, such as infants and protected persons, their human rights are protected through their guardians.)

### **Scope of Application of Condemnation**

**Academic Version**

Condemnation is applied only to **universal high crimes (large-scale killing of civilians, institutional persecution, repeated aggression).** Individual political issues and ideological conflicts are not subject to condemnation.

**Explanatory Version**

Condemnation is only for "high crimes that cross a line that is absolutely unforgivable for a human being, no matter who looks at it."

You will not be condemned for political conflicts or differences of opinion.

### **[Special and Most Important Note]**

**Academic Version**

The authority to declare condemnation and atonement belongs exclusively to the Global Council of Ethics. Any arrogation by any subject, including individuals, organizations, or AI, is considered a grave act of destruction against order itself, and each governing body has an absolute obligation to punish it according to law as soon as it is confirmed.

**Explanatory Version**

Only the Global Council of Ethics can declare "condemnation" or "atonement."

No individual, organization, or AI is ever permitted to say, "You are condemned."

If such an act is discovered, the governments of each country must punish it severely by law.

### **Priority of Culture, Religion, and Universal Human Rights (Connection with the Peoples Charter)**

**Academic Version**

The practices of each culture and religion are, in principle, respected to the greatest extent possible, but are rendered invalid if they conflict with universal human rights. The criterion for judgment is based on the **"List of Prohibited Practices"** attached to the THP Peoples Charter.

**Explanatory Version**

Diverse cultures and religions are respected.

However, if they conflict with universal human rights, which define what is "absolutely unforgivable for a human being," human rights take precedence over the rules of that culture or religion.

### **Articles**

#### **Article 1: Fundamental Principle - Affirmation of Life and Priority of Order**

**Academic Version**

The dignity of life is an absolute value, but that right is premised on the maintenance of the order that guarantees it.

**Explanatory Version**

Everyone's life is very important. However, that life and right are protected only on the foundation of "order."

#### **Article 2: Definition of Sin - Destruction of Order and Challenge to Human Morality**

**Academic Version**

Sin is limited to two things: (1) acts that intentionally destroy the framework for maintaining order, and (2) acts that violate human morality.

**Explanatory Version**

There are only two things that are sins:

-   Intentionally destroying the system that allows everyone to live in peace.
-   Crossing the line that should never be crossed as a human being.

#### **Article 3: Atonement - Reintegration into the International Community**

**Academic Version**

Atonement is the process of permitting reintegration into the international community on the condition that responsibility for the committed sin has been fully fulfilled and a framework for preventing recurrence has been established.

**Explanatory Version**

Atonement is the procedure by which one can return to the community as a member if one says "I'm sorry," makes proper amends, and a system is recognized to ensure that "it will never happen again."

#### **Article 4: Condemnation - Irreversible Historical Stigma**

**Academic Version**

Condemnation is the final and irreversible ethical judgment handed down in the name of humanity to a subject who has committed a universal high crime, and is a historical stigma.

**Explanatory Version**

Condemnation means that the fact that one has committed an "unforgivable sin" is permanently engraved in history.

#### **Article 5: Enforcement - E-MAD Protocol**

**Academic Version**

Sanctions accompanying condemnation and atonement are automatically enforced primarily by economic means, based on the E-MAD (Economically-Assured Mutual Destruction) protocol.

**Explanatory Version**

Those who break the rules are automatically punished by the economic mechanism of E-MAD.

"If you shoot, you will be shot. If you protect, you will be saved." - That is the basis of this order.

#### **Article 6: E-Penalty - Non-Violent Order Maintenance Measures**

**Academic Version**

E-Penalty is a micro-level order maintenance system in THP and is clearly distinguished from the atonement/condemnation framework of E-MAD. It applies to subjects who have violated their obligation to maintain order, and the measures applied are limited to non-violent means. Examples of measures include the addition of risk premiums in financial transactions, the lowering of various credit scores, and the lowering of priority in international cooperation networks. These are implemented through consultations between each governing body and the target country, based on the principles of **"transparency, fairness, and accuracy"** (where "accuracy" here refers to the balance between sin and punishment). If the deviant act exceeds the scope of E-Penalty, its handling is entrusted to E-MAD (National Councils of Ethics). THP acknowledges the existence of E-Penalty but is not involved in its direct implementation.

**Explanatory Version**

E-Penalty is a "small, non-violent punishment for rule violations."

For example, it is an **"economic and social disadvantage,"** such as making it harder to borrow money, lowering one's credit score, or being put at the back of the line in international cooperation.

If it escalates to a more serious violation (violence or serious destruction), E-MAD (the National Councils of Ethics) will make a judgment, and THP will not intervene.

### **Appendix Draft Article: "What is Condemnation (The Limits of THP)"**

#### **Chapter X: Condemnation and the Reality Precedence Clause**

1.  **Fundamental Principle**
    
    When human morality and reality conflict, reality is always deemed correct. Theories and ideals are merely tools to assist order and are never prioritized over reality.
    
2.  **Necessity of Application**
    
    In situations that shake the international order, such as large-scale state collapse, mass outflows of economic refugees, and other crises, it may seem ideal to accept everyone in accordance with human morality. However, if a single country were to "accept all," that country would immediately collapse, and the entire order would be lost. This article is established to avoid this self-destructive contradiction.
    
3.  **Definition of Condemnation**
    
    Condemnation is the irreversible judgment of an act or subject that has deviated from human morality. However, the judging party is also not permitted to deviate from human morality. The minimum exceptions that must be protected, such as women, children, and the seriously ill, are always respected.
    
4.  **Weight of Enforcement**
    
    Condemnation carries extremely grave meaning, and its arbitrary application corrupts order itself. Therefore, the final decision of condemnation always requires the deliberation of the **Humanity Study Group**, and serves as a guarantee against the psychological burden of the enforcer.
    
5.  **Vigilance Against Moral Hazard**
    
    It is inevitable that violators will attempt to use this exception provision for their own survival, on the premise of the existence of condemnation. Since the abuse of condemnation can inevitably occur, all application cases are audited and recorded, and measures are taken to prevent recurrence.
    
6.  **Positioning as a Limitation of THP**
    
    This article was not established to transcend human morality, but is a limitation provision to protect human morality. We are not agents of human morality, but merely observers and recorders for the maintenance of order. Therefore, the articles presented here are recorded at the end of the Ethics Charter and E-MAD materials as proof of the **"limits of order that humanity can achieve."**
