#### **Part 3: E-MAD Protocol Operational Case Study**

E-MAD is executed incrementally and automatically according to the severity of the violation.

-   **Stage 1: Financial Restriction**

    -   **Trigger:** People's Charter KPI (forced migration > 30,000 people), initial recognition of "universal high crime" by the Court of Ethics, etc.

    -   **Sanction Content:** Asset freezing of the target nation, organization, or individual, restriction of connection from international settlement networks (Fiat-Rev2), punitive increase in insurance premiums provided by the THP reinsurance pool.

    -   **Case Study:** A nation initiates a limited military invasion of a neighboring country. The Court of Ethics recognizes this as an "act of aggression." Immediately, the assets of the central bank and major financial institutions of that nation are frozen, making trade settlements difficult.

-   **Stage 2: Physical Conduit Severance**

    -   **Trigger:** Continued violation after sanctions, in case of humanitarian crisis.

    -   **Sanction Content:** JIMS member states physically stop the supply of strategic goods (energy, industrial parts, etc.) from the items covered by the lifeline plan (water, medical care, electricity, food), **excluding humanitarian essential medical care and food.**

    -   **Case Study:** The above nation continues its invasion, and a humanitarian crisis occurs in the occupied territory. Based on the decision of the JIMS Executive Council, exports of crude oil and semiconductors to that nation are completely stopped, paralyzing economic activity.

-   **Stage 3: Total Isolation**

    -   **Trigger:** Use of nuclear weapons, or large-scale inhumane acts equivalent thereto, confirmed.

    -   **Sanction Content:** All lifeline supplies are stopped, and UN-PDF physically blocks the borders of that nation. This is the final measure to completely cut off that nation from the framework of civilization.

#### **Part 4: Peace Fund Audit Flow and Ops-KPI Linkage**

The Peace Fund is a fund for humanitarian aid and reconstruction, financed by fines collected by E-MAD and revenues from frozen assets. Its operation is managed by an audit flow directly linked to the Ops-KPI dashboard to ensure absolute transparency.

1.  **Fund Generation:** When E-MAD is activated, target assets are automatically transferred to the Peace Fund account. All transaction records are recorded using blockchain technology, making them tamper-proof.

2.  **Decision on Fund Usage:** The usage of funds (e.g., food aid to a specific region) is decided by the JIMS Executive Council based on the recommendations of the Council of Ethics.

3.  **Execution and Audit:** The execution of aid is entrusted to specialized agencies such as WFP. The flow of aid materials is tracked in real-time using satellite data (AIS/SAR), etc., and that data is publicly disclosed in real-time as **"Humanitarian Aid KPI"** on the Ops-KPI dashboard.

4.  **Feedback:** The effectiveness of aid (e.g., improvement rate of nutrition) is evaluated by local third-party organizations, and the results are also disclosed on the dashboard. This prevents aid from being misused for political purposes.
