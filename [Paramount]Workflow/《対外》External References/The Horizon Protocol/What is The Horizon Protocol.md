#### E-MAD (Effective Multilateral Assured Denial) — Core Subsystem
- **Purpose**: Not retaliation, but denying the success of aggression in order to deter war and escalation.  
- **Human Dignity Lines**:  
  - *Red (Atonement)* — Invasion. Possible reintegration after reparations and obligations fulfilled.  
  - *Crimson (Condemnation)* — Nuclear use or crimes against humanity. Absolute condemnation, no forgiveness.  
- **Denial Principle**: Aggression is rendered irrational by ensuring its failure.  

**Operational Levels**  
- **Blue**: Normal. Education, monitoring.  
- **Yellow**: Attention. Diplomatic engagement.  
- **Orange**: Crisis. Non-punitive risk premiums applied.  
- **Red**: Confirmed aggression. Multilateral denial + ADI (Atonement Difficulty Index) for reintegration difficulty.  
- **Crimson**: Nuclear/humanitarian crime. Immediate denial of sovereign privileges, no return.  

**Key Indicators**  
- **E-MAD Score (0–100)**: Overall denial capacity.  
- **Estimated Loss**: Impact on aggressor, victim, third parties.  
- **ADI (0–100)**: Reintegration difficulty. 60+ = eligible for re-entry review.  
- **Transparency**: All models, data, code fully open.  

**Governance**  
- Tri-body structure: Experts Committee, Citizens Assembly, Human Ethics Council (sole adjudicator).  
- Four Sages: advisory only.  
  - Crimson: one retrial request.  
  - Red: one clemency request after ADI progress.  
- Proof standards: Red = high probability; Crimson = beyond reasonable doubt.  
- **Humanitarian Exception (Zero-Stop Principle)**: Medical, food, water, communications never cut.  

**Annex**  
- Color thresholds.  
- ADI weightings.  
- Risk premium formula.  
- Standard contract clauses (Crimson acceleration, Red rehabilitation).  
- Due process templates.  
- Four Sages’ rights.  
- Humanitarian exception details.
