## 1. Objective
Design an Indo-Pacific Strategic Infrastructure investment concept to diversify supply chains in the post-Walpurgis environment (Shin-Miyazawa 2.0 initiative).

## 2. Focus Areas
1. **Resilient Logistics Corridors**: Multi-modal ports, rail, and digital customs.
2. **Energy Transition Hubs**: LNG-to-hydrogen bridges, regional storage.
3. **Critical Tech Nodes**: Semiconductor, rare earth, and cloud infrastructure partnerships.

## 3. Principles
- Public-private co-financing with transparent governance.
- Alignment with THP MiniLateral commitments.
- Social and environmental safeguards (JICA/JBIC standards).

## 4. Next Steps
- Conduct stakeholder consultations with QUAD/ASEAN partners.
- Draft financial structure options (ODA, guarantees, blended finance).
