# THP-2: Ethics Charter

### Preamble

This Charter defines the ethical foundation of the new world order established by The Horizon Protocol (THP). Confronting the reality that conventional international law and theories of justice have become dysfunctional, it establishes the "maintenance of order" and the "defense of humanity" as absolute standards. All nations, organizations, and individuals shall be evaluated based on the provisions of this Charter, and the rightness of their actions shall be judged accordingly.

## Scope of Application

- Condemnation under this Charter is limited to universal grave crimes (large-scale killing of civilians, systemic persecution, repeated aggression). Individual political disputes are excluded.

## Priority of Culture/Religion and Universal Human Rights (Connection to the People Charter)

- Cultural and religious practices are protected, but in cases where they conflict with universal human rights, said practices shall be nullified. This is in accordance with the "Prohibited Practices List" of the People Charter.

### Article 1: Fundamental Principle: Affirmation of Life and Priority of Order

#### Research Version

All life is, in principle, affirmed in its existence. However, this affirmation is not unconditional; the maintenance of "order" as defined by THP is the highest priority. Human rights are not a natural endowment but are defined as acquired rights guaranteed for the first time by THP's order and the laws based on it. These rights may be restricted for any entity that threatens order.

#### Simplified Statutory Version
(A version that simplifies the main points of the research version into legal text. Terminology should be consistent with the Lexicon.)

Everyone's life is very important. However, the "order" that allows everyone to live in peace is even more important. The rights of a person are guaranteed for the first time by protecting this order.

### Article 2: Definition of Sin: Destruction of Order and Challenge to Humanity

#### Research Version

"Sin" in THP is broadly divided into the following two categories:

1.  **Acts that destroy order itself:** All acts that fundamentally undermine the foundations of global stability as defined by THP, such as inviting foreign aggression, destroying critical infrastructure through cyberattacks, and intentionally disrupting the key currency system.

2.  **Acts contrary to humanity:** Grave challenges to humanity, the foundation of civilization, such as genocide, the systematic massacre of defenseless civilians, intimidation by nuclear weapons, and the inhumane use of biological and chemical weapons.

#### Simplified Statutory Version
(A version that simplifies the main points of the research version into legal text. Terminology should be consistent with the Lexicon.)

In the world of THP, the following two things are "sins":

1.  Intentionally breaking the rules and systems that allow everyone to live in peace and security.

2.  Doing something that "one must never do as a human being" (→ see Lexicon: Humanity).

### Article 3: Atonement: Return to the International Community

#### Research Version

"Atonement" is the official process for clearing past sins and returning to the international community. This is not a mere pardon but is accompanied by the fulfillment of responsibility and a pledge to prevent recurrence. Approval is based on the procedures and sentencing of the **Court of Humanity** and is declared in the name of the **Council of Humanity**. The Four Sages are advisory (non-voting).

-   **Judicial Body:** The **Court of Humanity** is responsible for litigation procedures and sentencing, and the **final judgment is declared in the name of the Council of Humanity**.

#### Simplified Statutory Version
(A version that simplifies the main points of the research version into legal text. Terminology should be consistent with the Lexicon.)

"Atonement" (→ see Lexicon) means atoning for one's crimes, being forgiven by the world, and being allowed to rejoin the community.

### Article 4: Condemnation: The Irreversible Historical Brand

#### Research Version

"Condemnation" is the final ethical judgment rendered without room for salvation upon a subject who has committed a universal grave crime against humanity. The Court of Humanity is responsible for litigation procedures and sentencing, and the final judgment is declared in the name of the Council of Humanity (the Council of Humanity is responsible for deliberation and recommendations). Political condemnation is excluded (deliberation only when applicable to universal grave crimes). This condemnation is an irreversible historical brand and cannot be overturned by any change in era or political situation.

#### Simplified Statutory Version
(A version that simplifies the main points of the research version into legal text. Terminology should be consistent with the Lexicon.)

"Condemnation" (→ see Lexicon) means being eternally engraved in history as a sin that can never be forgiven.

### Article 5: Enforcement: E-MAD Protocol

#### Research Version

The enforcement of this Charter is, in principle, by non-violent economic and financial means, and its operation is in accordance with the "E-MAD (Effective Multilateral Assured Denial)" protocol¹. Sanctions by force are permissible only under strict conditions as a last resort against a condemned subject.
