### **Ethics Charter Supplementary Material: E-MAD Official Specifications (v1.2 Operational Version)**

#### **0. Preface and General Principles**

**Preface**
This document establishes the official specifications for **E-MAD (Effective Multilateral Assured Denial)** as a core operational subsystem of **The Horizon Protocol (THP)**. While the **Ethics Charter** provides an abstract and normative foundation (humanity, atonement, condemnation), this specification prioritizes **concreteness and rigor**. It defines how E-MAD is measured, executed, and governed, and ensures compatibility with each of THP's charters and operational dashboards.

E-MAD is a **non-violent, transparent, and multilateral** framework. Its purpose is not retaliation, but to **reliably deny the success of aggressors**, thereby preventing war and catastrophic escalation.

#### **1. Conceptual Foundation**
1.  **Humanity as a Boundary**: The line between *atonable* sins and sins that *should be condemned*.
2.  **Atonement** — *Red*: Acts of aggression. The subject can return to the international community after atonement and fulfillment of obligations. **Function**: Restorative and reintegrative.
3.  **Condemnation** — *Crimson*: Nuclear attacks, crimes against humanity, etc. Non-violent and absolute measures. **No forgiveness**, and the stigma is permanent. **Function**: Protective and exclusive.
4.  **Principle of Denial of Success**: To make the act of aggression itself **irrational**.

#### **2. E-MAD Hierarchical Response System**
Operates on a **5-level** scale.

-   **Blue (Baseline)**: Normal state. Continuous monitoring and citizen education.
-   **Yellow (Warning)**: Early warning. Diplomatic engagement.
-   **Orange (Critical Point)**: Crisis state. Multilateral diplomatic intervention and pressure through non-punitive **price signals** (risk premium additions).
-   **Red (Atonement)**: Act of aggression confirmed. Activation of **multifaceted denial-of-success measures** such as reparations, risk premiums, access restrictions, compliance investments, and monitoring obligations. A path to social reintegration is indicated based on the **Atonement Difficulty Index (ADI)**.
-   **Crimson (Condemnation)**: Nuclear attack or crime against humanity. Activation of absolute and non-violent measures such as the **termination of all debt privileges** (**one-strike-out**). The perpetrator's appeal is not permitted.

## Application to Ethnic Conflicts (Phased Sanctions)

- Phase 1: Increase in financing, settlement, and insurance rates (targeting the violating entity).
- Phase 2: Shut-off of pipelines for important goods (excluding medical care and food).
- Phase 3: Permanent corridor security by UN-PDF.
- The trigger conditions are linked to the KPIs of the People-Charter, and the final judgment is in the name of the Council of Ethics.

#### **3. Metrics**
1.  **E-MAD Score (0–100)**: A composite index that aggregates denial capabilities in multiple domains.
2.  **Estimated Loss**: Quantifies the losses of the aggressor, victim, and third parties in monetary terms.
3.  **Atonement Difficulty Index (ADI, 0–100)**: A multifaceted index that measures the difficulty of social reintegration in Red cases.
4.  **Transparency Requirements**: All data, models, and code will be open-sourced and subject to independent audits.

#### **4. Governance and Process**

1.  **Tripartite Governance**:
    -   **Expert Committee**: Methodology, models, quality control.
    -   **Citizens' Assembly**: Ensuring legitimacy through deliberation.
    -   **Council of Ethics**: The **sole adjudicating body** for E-MAD phase decisions.

2.  **The Four Sages — Advisory Body**:
    -   **No voting rights. Not a member of the Council of Ethics**.
    -   **Crimson Cases**: Can exercise a one-time **right to request reconsideration**.
    -   **Red Cases**: Can exercise a one-time **right to request a special pardon** after substantial progress has been made on the ADI.
    -   All opinions are published as public memoranda, and the final decision belongs to the Council of Ethics.

3.  **Due Process, Standard of Evidence, and Appeals**:
    -   **Standard of Evidence**: Red = **high probability**, Crimson = **beyond a reasonable doubt**.
    -   **Appeals**: In Crimson cases, the perpetrator's appeal is not possible. In Red cases, it is possible based on ADI targets.
    -   **Humanitarian Exception (Zero-Stop Principle)**: Medical care, food, water, and relief communications will never be stopped.

### **Annexes**

#### **Annex A — Color Classification Thresholds**
E-MAD Score Range (Initial proposal. Updated quarterly via public RFC)
-   **Blue**: ≥ 80
-   **Yellow**: 65–79
-   **Orange**: 50–64
-   **Red**: < 50 (However, immediate transition to Red upon "aggression/attack execution" trigger)
-   **Crimson**: Applied immediately regardless of score when the humanity condemnation trigger (nuclear/genocide, etc.) is activated.

#### **Annex B — ADI (Atonement Difficulty Index) Initial Weights**
Vectors and Weights (Total = 1.00)
-   a. Reparation Capital: 0.30
-   b. Risk Premium Coefficient × Period: 0.20
-   c. Access Restriction Period: 0.15
-   d. Compliance Verification: 0.15
-   e. Victim Relief: 0.15
-   f. Minimization of Humanitarian Impact: 0.05
*Aggregation: Weighted geometric mean of normalized indicators → ADI. A score of 60 or higher allows for re-entry review.*

#### **Annex C — Risk Premium Formula for Orange State**
Proposed coefficients: `RP = β0 + β1·ESG_risk + β2·SupplyFrag + β3·SanctionProb`
*Updated weekly. β coefficients are determined by public RFC, and the regression formula and dataset are open-sourced.*

#### **Annex D — Standard Contract Clauses (Model Clauses)**
-   **[Crimson Acceleration Clause]**
    "In the event of a Crimson determination under the E-MAD Protocol, with the exception of the humanitarian zero-stop exception, all debt privileges shall terminate and all outstanding debts shall become immediately due and payable. The parties confirm that this decision will be mutually recognized in all participating jurisdictions."
-   **[Red Rehabilitation Clause]**
    "Red cases shall be subject to risk premium additions, temporary access restrictions, and compliance investments through third-party escrow until the ADI threshold is met under independent monitoring."

#### **Annex E — Due Process Forms (Templates)**
-   Notification of Determination (Orange/Red/Crimson): Summary of facts / Summary of evidence / Applicable articles / Remedies
-   Set of Evidentiary Documents: Data source, timestamp, verification code
-   Appeal Form (Red only): Grounds for appeal / Additional evidence / Corrective proposals
-   Memorandum for Reconsideration (Four Sages): One-time / Public opinion paper

#### **Annex F — Authority Protocol of the Four Sages (Distinction)**
-   No voting rights. Institutionally separate from the Council of Ethics.
-   Crimson: Right to request reconsideration (one-time, submission → publication → Council deliberation).
-   Red: Right to request a special pardon (one-time, after reaching ADI progress).
-   All are archived as public memorandums.

#### **Annex G — Humanitarian Zero-Stop (Operation of Humanitarian Exception)**
-   **Scope**: Medical care, food, water, relief communications, logistics for vaccines/essential medicines.
-   **Implementation**: Permanent whitelist and third-party audits. A design goal (KPI) of zero suspension events.

## Note on Organizational Alignment (Council / Court / Four Sages)

- The final judgment is in the name of the Council of Ethics. The Court of Ethics is the judicial branch.
- The Four Sages are advisory (no vote). This specification shall be consistent with the THP-ORF / Ethics Charter.
