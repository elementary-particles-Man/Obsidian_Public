# THP-4: Religion Charter

### Preamble: Coexistence of Religion and Reality

#### Research Version

The Horizon Protocol (THP) redefines religion as something belonging to the realm of an individual's inner spirituality, completely separate from the state and politics. The basis for coexistence lies in positioning **"reality,"** which is empirical and verifiable, as a common frame of reference above all religions and ideologies. Religions have the freedom to speak of hope and meaning in their respective doctrines, based on this common reality.

#### Simplified Statutory Version

Mutual respect for each other's faith. However, no faith shall deny scientific or medical facts, nor justify acts that harm the life or health of others.

### Article 1: Freedom of Faith and Separation of Church and State

- (Reference) Lexicon: Cult / Inviolability of Science and Medicine.

#### Research Version

All individuals have the right to freely choose, explore, practice, or abandon their own faith. The state shall not favor or suppress any particular religion, and bears the duty to maintain a thorough separation of church and state in public education and administration.

#### Simplified Statutory Version

1.  It is entirely up to the individual whether or not to believe in any religion.

2.  The state and government shall not favor or bully any particular religion.

### Article 2: Duty of Mutual Respect

- (Reference) Lexicon: Cult / Inviolability of Science and Medicine.

#### Research Version

All believers have a duty to respect the faith and ideas of others. The act of asserting the absoluteness of one's own doctrine and insulting or attacking the faith of others as "error" or "evil" is "intolerance" prohibited by THP and is considered an act that destroys the spiritual peace of the world.

#### Simplified Statutory Version

3.  You must not make fun of other people's faith or decide that it is wrong.

### Article 3: Prohibition of Falsehoods Against Reality

- (Reference) Lexicon: Cult / Inviolability of Science and Medicine.

#### Research Version

Acts that deny scientific or medical reality, performed in the name of religion, and the act of forcing them on others, are not faith but "falsehoods" and are prohibited¹.

#### Simplified Statutory Version

4.  You must not tell "lies" that deny science or medicine for religious reasons, or force them on others.

### Article 4: Definition and Exclusion of Cults

- (Reference) Lexicon: Cult / Inviolability of Science and Medicine.

#### Research Version

Organizations that deny the faith of others, encourage isolation from society, or exploit believers mentally or economically are defined not as religions but as "cults" and are subject to exclusion under the THP Ethics Charter.

#### Simplified Statutory Version

5.  Dangerous groups that deceive people for money or isolate them from society are not religions but just "cults." They are not permitted.

### Article 5: Salvation through Reality

- (Reference) Lexicon: Cult / Inviolability of Science and Medicine.

#### Research Version

In the worldview of THP, "reality" already provides universal and neutral salvation through all the phenomena of the universe. Religion has the role of speaking of individual hopes and meanings on the foundation of that reality.

#### Simplified Statutory Version

6.  "Reality," such as water, the sun, and the earth, allows all people to live equally. Religion is built on gratitude for that.

¹ _For details, see THP-5: Lexicon (sections on "Cult" and "Inviolability of Science and Medicine")._
