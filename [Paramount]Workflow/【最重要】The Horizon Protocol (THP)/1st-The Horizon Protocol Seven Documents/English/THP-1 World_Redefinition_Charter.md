# World Redefinition Charter

## Detailed Version

### Preamble: Death Certificate for the Old World
The "national narratives" and "ethnic myths" forged in the 20th century have begun to self-destruct under the weight of their internal contradictions and historical deceptions. Japan's "international contribution," Great Britain's "faded glory," Italy's "historical reconciliation," and Israel's "promised land" are all based on self-serving historical interpretations. By avoiding a direct confrontation with their difficult pasts, they have invited the severe global conflicts and dysfunctions of today.

In contrast, Germany has presented a "narrative of responsibility." By not turning away from its own horrific past, integrating it into the core of its national identity, and shouldering it as a permanent responsibility, the German people were able to build a foundation of trust in post-war Europe. The truth this comparison reveals is singular: "A narrative that avoids a difficult past will always fail."

In the post-Walpurgis world, myths and selective interpretations of history no longer have the power to bind the world order. The sole basis for the new order is the universal and transparent rule shown in the THP Ethics Charter: "Those who destroy order and trample on humanity shall be punished." This declaration is a death certificate for the narratives of the old world and a birth certificate for the new order led by THP.

### Article 1: Primacy of Universal Order
The one and only absolute justice in the new world is the "maintenance of order." No act that destroys order itself or transgresses against humanity can be justified, no matter the pretext—be it ethnicity, religion, nation, or ideology. A legitimate member of the world, regardless of its size or economic power, is any entity that respects this universal order and bears the responsibility for its maintenance.

### Article 2: Integration of Responsibility and Atonement
Sins of the past cannot be dissolved through forgetfulness or relativization. They must be permanently integrated into the identity of a nation or people as "responsibility." As the example of Germany shows, it is the inheritance of responsibility that builds trust in the future. THP, on the premise of the fulfillment of this responsibility, establishes a gateway for "atonement" that makes possible a return to the international community.

### Article 3: Irreversibility of Condemnation
Acts defined as "universal grave crimes" by the THP Ethics Charter—such as war crimes, genocide, and crimes against humanity—are to be "condemned" as an ethical judgment by all of humanity, transcending political considerations. This condemnation is an irreversible historical brand that cannot be overturned by any change in era or political situation. This is humanity's pledge to the future.

### Article 4: Horizontality of Peoples and Religions
All peoples and religions are equal and horizontal in their culture, history, and values; no hierarchy exists. Their diversity is to be respected and protected as an asset of all humanity. However, when a group denies others, proclaims its own superiority, or justifies violence, it is no longer considered a people or a religion, but a "cult" or "instrument of violence" to be condemned by THP. The basis for coexistence lies in the mutual recognition that "your righteousness and my righteousness are both right."

### Article 5: Scientific Realism
Science is not omnipotent, but as "knowledge of reality" based on objective data and verifiable processes, it is to be placed at the foundation of all decision-making. In medicine especially, standard treatment based on scientific evidence is considered the best option, and obstructing it for religious or cultural reasons is deemed a grave infringement upon life.

### Article 6: Co-creation of Future Narratives
The narrative of "justice," once monopolized by nations, peoples, and religions in the old world, has lost its power. The only narrative that can weave the post-Walpurgis future is the process itself, in which all entities collaborate to create a common order under the three pillars of "transparency, fairness, and accuracy" upheld by THP.

### Epilogue: An Evolving Protocol
The seven documents of THP, including this declaration, are not a completed scripture. They are an evolving protocol that future generations should constantly rewrite through democratic processes to match the realities of their time. Our goal is not the realization of a perfect world, but the perpetual, unceasing effort to pursue a world where all life can maintain its dignity and enjoy peace in any era.

## Simplified Version

- **The end of old stories:** Self-serving historical interpretations and myths are no longer valid. Countries that turn away from their past will fail.
- **The new rule:** There is only one rule for the world: "Punish those who destroy order and those who do what is unforgivable as human beings."
- **Sin and responsibility:** Do not forget past sins; continue to accept them as responsibility. That is what leads to trust in the future.
- **Peoples and religions:** All peoples and religions are equal; there is no superiority or inferiority. The basic principle is "yours is right, and mine is right, too."
- **Science is important:** Trust data and facts over assumptions. Especially in medicine, scientific standard treatment is best.
- **How to make the future:** We will create a new story together, with "transparency, fairness, and accuracy" as the rules.
- **This rule isn't perfect either:** It's okay for future generations to change this declaration. The important thing is to keep aiming for a peaceful world.
