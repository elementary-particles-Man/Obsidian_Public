# People-Charter

## Detailed Version

### Preamble: Redefining Peoples
The history of the 20th century has been a repeating tragedy of peoples being linked to "nations" in the name of nationalism, justifying superiority over others and becoming a source of conflict. The Horizon Protocol (THP) declares that it will right this wrong and liberate peoples from the yoke of nations and territories. A people in this Charter is defined as a human group that shares a history, culture, and language, and serves as a source of identity and spiritual support for individuals. All peoples, regardless of their size or the length of their history, are to be respected as equal and horizontal beings.

### Article 1: Freedom of Ethnic Affiliation
All individuals have the right to freely choose the people with whom they feel a sense of belonging and to inherit their culture and pride. Ethnic identity is not something to be enforced by birth or lineage, but is a part of the right to self-determination based on individual dignity. The state shall not infringe upon this freedom for any reason whatsoever.

### Article 2: Protection of Culture and Tradition
The unique cultures, languages, and traditions that each people has nurtured are the intangible assets of all humanity. THP will support the protection and succession of these assets when they are in danger of extinction. However, this does not apply if the tradition conflicts with the THP Ethics Charter (e.g., by infringing on human rights).

### Article 3: Duty of Mutual Respect and Prohibition of Discrimination and Attack
People belonging to different peoples have a duty to respect each other's values and history. Any act that seeks to establish the identity of one people through attacks, discrimination, or the distortion or denial of the history of another people is considered a grave violation that destroys the world order.

### Article 4: Complete Denial of Supremacist Ideology
No people shall claim to be superior to or more special than any other people. All forms of ethnic supremacist ideology based on historical circumstances, economic power, cultural influence, etc., are the root of the tragedies of the 20th century and will never be tolerated in the world of THP. There is no hierarchy among peoples, only horizontal diversity.

### Article 5: The Principle of Coexistence and the Degeneration into Violence
A people functions as a positive community that saves individuals from loneliness and nurtures a rich culture. However, when that energy is directed toward hatred of other peoples or the plunder of resources, it is no longer considered a people but is judged to have degenerated into an "instrument of violence" to be condemned under the THP Ethics Charter.

### Epilogue
This Charter is a guidepost for transforming peoples from instruments of conflict into a foundation that celebrates cultural diversity and supports the coexistence of humanity.

## Simplified Version

- **Peoples are free and equal:** All peoples are equal, with no hierarchy. You can decide your own people.
- **Culture is everyone's treasure:** The cultures and languages of various peoples are to be treasured as the common heritage of all humanity.
- **Respect each other:** It is absolutely forbidden to make fun of, attack, or discriminate against other peoples.
- **"We are the best" is prohibited:** Thinking that "our people alone are special" is the source of conflict and is prohibited.
- **No violence:** A people must not be used as a tool to harm others.

## Footnote: Authority of the Four Sages
The Four Sages are an **advisory body (non-voting)** and have **no voting rights** in the PMC and humanitarian organizations.

## Organizational Alignment (Council/Court/Four Sages)
- **The final judgment is in the name of the Council of Humanity.**
- Court of Humanity: Judicial (litigation procedures and sentencing).
- The Four Sages: **Advisory (non-voting)**.

## Operational Principles (Order of Priority)
- This Charter guarantees "freedom of ethnic affiliation and protection of culture." However, priority is given in the order of universal human rights (Ethics Charter) > religious and cultural customs > administrative discretion.
- The rights of the individual take precedence over the rights of the group (including the protection of children, women, and minorities).
- The three principles of intervention: minimum necessity, proportionality, and reversibility.

## Examination and Execution Procedures for National Self-Determination (Operational Protocol)
1) Acceptance of petition: Only petitions that meet the requirements for representativeness, denial of violence, and respect for basic human rights are subject to examination.
2) Protective measures: A provisional order prohibiting retaliation and suspending forced migration can be issued at the same time as the petition.
3) Impact assessment: Integrated assessment of population, living areas, infrastructure, and finances (within 60 days).
4) Design of options: Presentation of phased proposals for a. cultural autonomy / b. broad autonomy (finance and education) / c. federalization / d. separation and independence.
5) Confirmation of the will of the residents: Fair voting under international supervision (two rounds of voting: draft proposal → final proposal).
6) Provisional autonomy: The rules for the transitional government, joint security, and apportionment of shared assets are finalized in a provisional agreement.
7) Final peace agreement: Human rights clauses, protection of minorities, and cross-border traffic (residence and commerce) are treaty-based.
8) Supervision: Monitored by JIMS/ICPO-2. Serious violations are subject to E-MAD and UN-PDF.

## Protocol for the Settlement of Historical Hatred (TRC + Reparations)
- Truth and Reconciliation Commission (TRC): Fact-finding on perpetration and damage, preservation of testimony, public hearings.
- Restorative measures: A combination of restitution, alternative compensation, and joint management (of holy sites, water rights, and cemeteries).
- Inviolable corridors: Application of non-aggression to religious, medical, and educational facilities. Violations trigger E-MAD Stage 2 (blocking of funds, insurance, and distribution).
- Time-limited supervision: Evaluation every three years; sanctions are strengthened if there is no improvement.

## Boundary between the Protection of Culture and Universal Human Rights (List of Prohibited Practices)
- Absolutely prohibited: Child marriage / female genital mutilation (FGM) / honor killings / forced conversion or punishment for apostasy / slave-like labor / corporal punishment.
- Conditionally permitted: Clothing, dietary rules, and rituals are protected to the extent that they do not violate individual freedom.
- Enforcement: Violations are dealt with in the order of domestic law → ICPO-2 cross-border inquiry → Court of Humanity → E-MAD.

## Case Study Template (Operational Template)
- Case A: Overlapping claims of settlers and indigenous peoples
  1) Immediate ceasefire and inviolable corridor → 2) Joint management → 3) Clause for shelving ownership rights → 4) Optional system of phased return/compensation.
- Case B: Request for separation from a multi-ethnic autonomous region
  1) Examination of acceptance requirements → 2) Broad autonomy (finance and education) → 3) Internationally monitored referendum → 4) Final treaty on federalization or independence.
- Case C: Conflict between religious customs and child protection
  1) Emergency protection order → 2) Cultural substitution (replacement of symbols) → 3) Supervised compliance plan → 4) Graduated sanctions for violations.

## KPI and Escalation Conditions (Connection to Ops-KPI)
- Forced migration: >10,000 in 7 days = alert. >30,000 = E-MAD Stage 1 (financial restrictions).
- Civilian casualties: >5 per 100,000 per week = alert. >20 = UN-PDF dispatch review.
- Attacks on religious facilities: 1 incident = warning, 3 consecutive incidents = E-MAD Stage 1 + ICPO-2 international wanted list.
- Non-compliance with agreements: Two consecutive negative evaluations automatically trigger freezing of funds and increase in insurance premiums.

## Organizational Arrangement and Authority (People's Mediation Committee / PMC)
- PMC (People's Mediation Committee): A specialized committee under JIMS. Cases are examined by subcommittees on human rights, finance, geopolitics, and religion.
- Authority: Recommendation of provisional orders, establishment of TRCs, design of referendums. The final judgment is in the name of the Council of Humanity, and judicial procedures are handled by the Court of Humanity.
- Cooperation: ICPO-2 (cross-border inquiries of violators), UN-PDF (corridor security), New Financial Order Council (implementation of sanctions).
