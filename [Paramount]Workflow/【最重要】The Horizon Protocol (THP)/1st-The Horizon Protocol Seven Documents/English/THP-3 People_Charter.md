# People-Charter

## Detailed Version

### Preamble: Redefining Ethnicity
The history of the 20th century has been a tragic repetition of ethnicities being linked to "nations" in the name of nationalism, justifying superiority over others, and becoming a hotbed of conflict. The Horizon Protocol (THP) declares to rectify this error and liberate ethnicity from the yoke of nation and territory. In this Charter, ethnicity is defined as a human group that shares history, culture, and language, serving as an individual's identity and spiritual anchor. All ethnicities, regardless of their size or historical duration, are respected as equally horizontal entities.

### Article 1: Freedom of Ethnic Affiliation
Every individual has the right to freely choose the ethnicity they identify with and to inherit its culture and pride. Ethnic identity is not to be coerced by birth or lineage but is part of an individual's self-determination based on dignity. The state shall not infringe upon this freedom for any reason.

### Article 2: Protection of Culture and Tradition
The unique cultures, languages, and traditions nurtured by each ethnicity are intangible assets of all humanity. THP supports the protection and inheritance of these assets when they are at risk of extinction. However, this does not apply if such traditions conflict with the THP Ethical Charter (e.g., infringing upon human rights).

### Article 3: Duty of Mutual Respect and Prohibition of Discrimination/Aggression
People belonging to different ethnicities have a duty to respect each other's values and history. Acts that attempt to establish one's ethnic identity through aggression, discrimination, or distortion/denial of the history of other ethnicities are considered serious violations that destroy world order.

### Article 4: Complete Rejection of Supremacist Ideologies
No ethnicity shall claim to be superior or special to other ethnicities. All forms of ethnic supremacist ideology, based on historical circumstances, economic power, cultural influence, etc., are the root cause of 20th-century tragedies and are never tolerated in the THP world. There is no hierarchy among ethnicities; there is only horizontal diversity.

### Article 5: Principle of Coexistence and Transformation into Violence
Ethnicity functions as a positive community that saves individuals from loneliness and fosters rich culture. However, when its energy is directed towards hatred of other ethnicities or exploitation of resources, it is no longer considered an ethnicity but is judged to have transformed into an "instrument of violence" that should be condemned based on the THP Ethical Charter.

### Conclusion
This Charter is a guide to transform ethnicity from a tool of conflict into a foundation that celebrates cultural diversity and supports human coexistence.

## Simplified Version

- **Ethnicity is Free and Equal**: All ethnicities are equal, with no hierarchy. Individuals can choose their own ethnicity.
- **Culture is a Treasure for All**: The cultures and languages of various ethnicities are treasures for all humanity and should be cherished.
- **Respect Each Other**: It is absolutely forbidden to belittle, attack, or discriminate against other ethnicities.
- **"We are the Best" is Forbidden**: The idea that "our ethnicity is special" is a source of conflict and is forbidden.
- **Violence is Not Allowed**: Ethnicity should not be used as a tool to harm others.

## Footnote: Authority of the Four Sages
The Four Sages are an **advisory body (non-voting)** and **do not have voting rights** in the PMC or human morality institutions.

## Organizational Integration (Councils / Courts / Four Sages)
- **Final judgment is in the name of the Council of Human Morality**.
- Court of Human Morality: Judiciary (litigation procedures and sentencing).
- Four Sages: **Advisory (non-voting)**.

## Operational Principles (Priorities)
- This Charter guarantees "freedom of ethnic affiliation and protection of culture." However, universal human rights (Ethical Charter) > religious/cultural practices > administrative discretion are prioritized in that order.
- Individual rights take precedence over collective rights (including the protection of children, women, and minorities).
- Three principles of intervention: minimum necessary, proportionality, and reversibility.

## Ethnic Self-Determination Review and Enforcement Procedures (Operational Protocol)
1) Acceptance of Petition: Only petitions meeting acceptance criteria (representation, non-violence, adherence to fundamental human rights) are reviewed.
2) Interim Measures: Provisional orders prohibiting "retaliation and forced displacement" can be issued concurrently with the petition.
3) Impact Assessment: Integrated assessment of population, living areas, infrastructure, and finances (within 60 days).
4) Option Design: Presentation of phased proposals: a. Cultural autonomy / b. Broad regional autonomy (finance, education) / c. Federalization / d. Separation and independence.
5) Confirmation of Resident Intent: Fair referendum under international supervision (two rounds of voting: conceptual proposal → final proposal).
6) Provisional Autonomy: Interim government, joint security, and rules for proportional distribution of shared assets are established by provisional agreement.
7) Final Reconciliation: Human rights clauses, minority protection, and cross-border movement (residence, commerce) are codified into a treaty.
8) Oversight: Monitored by JIMS/ICPO-2. Serious violations are subject to E-MAD and UN-PDF.

## Protocol for Clearing Historical Hatred (TRC + Reparations)
- Truth and Reconciliation Commission (TRC): Fact-finding of perpetrators and victims, preservation of testimonies, public hearings.
- Restorative Measures: Combination of restitution, alternative compensation, and joint management (holy sites, water resources, cemeteries).
- Inviolable Corridors: Military inviolability applied to religious facilities, medical facilities, and educational institutions. Violations trigger E-MAD Stage 2 (disruption of funding, insurance, and distribution).
- Time-Limited Oversight: Evaluation every 3 years; intensified sanctions if no improvement.

## Boundary Between Cultural Protection and Universal Human Rights (List of Prohibited Practices)
- Absolutely Prohibited: Child marriage / Female Genital Mutilation (FGM) / Honor killings / Forced conversion or apostasy penalties / Slave labor / Corporal punishment.
- Conditionally Permitted: Clothing, dietary rules, and rituals are protected as long as they do not infringe upon individual freedom.
- Enforcement: Violations are handled in order: domestic law → ICPO-2 cross-border referral → Court of Human Morality → E-MAD.

## Case Study Template (Operational Template)
- Case A: Overlapping claims of settlements and indigenous peoples
  1) Immediate ceasefire, inviolable corridors → 2) Joint management → 3) Moratorium on ownership → 4) Optional phased return/compensation.
- Case B: Secession request from a multi-ethnic autonomous region
  1) Review of acceptance criteria → 2) Broad regional autonomy (finance, education) → 3) Internationally supervised referendum → 4) Final treaty for federalization or independence.
- Case C: Conflict between religious customs and child protection
  1) Emergency protection order → 2) Cultural alternative (symbolic replacement) → 3) Supervised compliance plan → 4) Escalation of sanctions in case of violation.

## KPI and Escalation Conditions (Connection to Ops-KPI)
- Forced Displacement: Over 10,000 people in 7 days = alert. Over 30,000 people = E-MAD Stage 1 (financial restrictions).
- Civilian Casualties: Over 5 incidents per 100,000 people per week = alert. Over 20 incidents = UN-PDF deployment review.
- Attacks on Religious Facilities: 1 incident = caution, 3 consecutive incidents = E-MAD Stage 1 + ICPO-2 international warrant.
- Non-compliance with Agreement: 2 consecutive periods of unsatisfactory oversight evaluation = automatic activation of fund freezing and increased insurance premiums.

## Organizational Placement and Authority (Ethnic Mediation Committee / PMC)
- PMC (Ethnic Mediation Committee): A specialized committee under JIMS. Reviews cases in subcommittees for human rights, finance, geopolitics, and religion.
- Authority: Recommendation of provisional orders, establishment of TRC, design of referendums. Final judgment is in the name of the Council of Human Morality, judicial procedures are handled by the Court of Human Morality.
- Collaboration: ICPO-2 (cross-border referral of violators), UN-PDF (corridor security), New Financial Order Council (sanction implementation).
