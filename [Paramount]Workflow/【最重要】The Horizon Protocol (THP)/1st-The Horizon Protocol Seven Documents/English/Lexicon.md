# Lexicon

### Preamble
This Lexicon defines and explains the key concepts, abbreviations, and technical terms used throughout the seven documents that constitute The Horizon Protocol (THP). To accurately understand the philosophy and structure of THP, cross-referencing with each document is recommended. This lexicon will be updated sequentially as THP evolves.

---

## The Horizon Protocol (THP)

**Detailed Version**
Formerly known as Ark-R (Ark-Restructure/Ark-Redefinition). A comprehensive framework for redesigning the post-Walpurgis world order across four layers: "financial plumbing," "life infrastructure," "ethical norms," and "national narratives," assuming the U.S. debt crisis on 2025/09/30 as the critical point. The central concept is the formation of a "transparent, fair, and accurate" order, aiming for a balance based on symmetrical non-identity rather than the hegemony of a specific nation.

**Simplified Version**
THP is "a plan to rebuild the world's order based on transparent, fair, and accurate rules."

---

## LSC (Libra/Scales Capitalism)

**Detailed Version**
An abbreviation for Libra/Scales Capitalism. It is a "general-purpose reality improvement support system" that forms the philosophical basis of THP. Its ultimate goal is the "coexistence of financial markets and the real economy," and it continuously cycles through "starting from reality and aiming to improve reality." Its main feature is being "variably adaptive," constantly transforming itself to fit reality without adhering to fixed doctrines.

**Simplified Version**
LSC is a way of thinking to "stop doing troublesome things." It's a thinking tool to keep solving real problems in practical ways.

---

## Walpurgis

**Detailed Version**
In a narrow sense, it refers to the symbolic event of a de facto U.S. default: the failure of the primary dealer auction for U.S. Treasury bonds on September 30, 2025. In a broader sense, it is used as a general term for the complex global crises that could occur in a chain reaction, including mass layoffs due to AI, a real estate crisis in China, financial instability in Europe, and a collapse of global food security.

**Simplified Version**
Walpurgis is "the day around September 30, 2025, when the American financial system breaks, triggering a simultaneous global chaos."

---

## Atonement

**Detailed Version**
An official process stipulated in the THP Ethics Charter for clearing past sins and returning to the international community. It is established by a joint ruling of the symbols of civilization (the Emperor of Japan, the Pope, the House of Saud, the British Monarch) and the Humanitarian Court. It is not a mere pardon but involves the fulfillment of responsibilities and a pledge to prevent recurrence.

**Simplified Version**
When the world acknowledges your "sorry" and lets you back in the group, on the condition that you fulfill your responsibilities.

---

## Condemnation

**Detailed Version**
A final ethical judgment, without possibility of salvation, rendered upon a subject who has committed a universal great sin against humanity, as stipulated in the THP Ethics Charter. It is established when a judgment by the Humanitarian Court is certified by the symbols of civilization (the four holy figures) and is recorded as an irreversible historical mark.

**Simplified Version**
To be etched into history forever as a crime that can never be forgiven. It is humanity's final declaration.

---

## Humanitarian Court

**Detailed Version**
The supreme judicial body established under the New United Nations (UN rev.2) responsible for enforcing the THP Ethics Charter. It adjudicates on "Atonement" and "Condemnation." Its judgments are based on pure ethical standards, independent of the political interests of any nation.

**Simplified Version**
The world's highest court that decides what is permissible and what is not, based on the THP rulebook (the Ethics Charter).

---

## International Police and Criminal Organization (ICPO-2)

**Detailed Version**
A supranational agency that investigates and enforces "sins" across borders based on the THP Ethics Charter. It has jurisdiction over five domains—physical, economic, information, humanitarian, and order-destruction—and no national sovereignty can impede its activities.

**Simplified Version**
The world's most powerful police force that catches bad guys, ignoring borders.

---

## United Nations Peace Defense Force (UN-PDF)

**Detailed Version**
An abbreviation for United Nations Peace Defense Force. A multinational force under the New United Nations (UN rev.2), with the U.S. military and the Japan Self-Defense Forces at its core, responsible for preventing armed conflicts and carrying out military sanctions against condemned subjects. It is the power that implements the "if you shoot, be prepared to be shot" principle in the international order.

**Simplified Version**
The new UN's army for stopping wars before they start and punishing countries that commit major sins against the world.

---

## New United Nations (UN rev.2)

**Detailed Version**
A new international governing body restructured from the former United Nations based on the principles of THP. It oversees the Humanitarian Court, ICPO-2, and the UN-PDF, and systematically operates the dual mechanisms of Atonement and Condemnation. Its authority is limited to addressing the "universal great sins" defined in the Ethics Charter.

**Simplified Version**
A powered-up version of the old UN. It commands the world police and world army to protect global peace and rules.

---

## E-MAD

**Detailed Version**
An abbreviation for Economic-Mutually Assured Deterrence. A mechanism of mutually assured deterrence that automatically reflects the costs back onto a state that uses its economy or finance as a weapon, through immediate measures such as increased insurance rates or disconnection from financial networks.

**Simplified Version**
A system where "if you punch someone economically, you automatically get punched back." A deterrent to economic warfare.

---

## KPI (Key Performance Indicator)

**Detailed Version**
An abbreviation for Key Performance Indicator. Quantitative evaluation metrics set in various fields such as finance, supply chains, healthcare, food, and infrastructure to monitor the operational status of THP. When a specific threshold (trigger) is crossed, automatic countermeasures, such as the activation of swaps, are executed.

**Simplified Version**
A tool to measure "if things are being done right" with numbers. If the numbers go above or below a set value, an alarm sounds and countermeasures begin automatically.

---

## Discrepancy Index

**Detailed Version**
A THP-specific KPI that quantifies the "density of contradictions and discrepancies" among multiple information sources, such as government announcements, official reports, and market data. It objectively detects the collapse of national narratives and propaganda.

**Simplified Version**
A number that represents the degree of suspiciousness, as in "something isn't adding up here." It's an alarm for finding lies and deception.

---

## Cult

**Detailed Version**
A group that deviates from the freedom of religion, infringes on the human rights of its followers or third parties, denies the faith of others, obstructs science and standard medical care, and idolizes the private interests of its leader. In the THP Religion Charter, it is defined as "violence, not religion."

**Simplified Version**
A violent group pretending to be a religion that hurts people, doesn't accept other ideas, and gets in the way of science and medicine.
