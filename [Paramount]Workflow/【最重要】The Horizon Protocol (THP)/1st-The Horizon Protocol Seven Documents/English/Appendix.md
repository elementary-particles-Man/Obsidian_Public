# Appendix: The Philosophy of LSC

## Detailed Version

### Preamble: LSC - The Operating System for Reality Improvement
LSC (Libra/Scales Capitalism) is the "thinking operating system" that underlies the entire design philosophy of The Horizon Protocol (THP). It includes the name "Capitalism" because its ultimate goal is the "coexistence and balance of financial markets and the real economy," but its essence is not an ideology or a doctrine. LSC is a "general-purpose thinking support system" for continuously improving the problems of a complex and contradictory reality by practical means.

Unlike other "-isms" that aim to achieve a fixed "ideal state," LSC has no such goal. Its sole principle of action is to "stop wasteful and unproductive conflicts and waste (= troublesome things)." Therefore, LSC is not immutable; it is a philosophy of "variable adaptability" that constantly changes its own form in response to changes in reality.

For its practice, LSC has a core concept consisting of the following seven axioms. Understanding these seven axioms and applying them to real-world problems (module construction) is the entirety of LSC.

### The Seven Axioms of LSC

1.  **Axiom 1: The Abnormal is Precious**
    The normal state, because it is stable, has little fluctuation in value. In contrast, the "abnormal"—such as peace (where war is the norm), life (where death is the norm), and wakefulness (where sleep is the norm)—is an extremely rare and temporary miracle, and therefore overwhelmingly valuable. What we must protect is this precious "abnormality."

2.  **Axiom 2: Form is Emptiness, Emptiness is Form (Nothing is Everything)**
    All things are in flux, and no value is eternal. "Nothing" in one situation holds the potential for "infinity" in another. Just as hope is born from the depths of despair, and innovation is born from a blank slate, 0 and ∞ are equivalent. This recognition frees us from fixed ideas.

3.  **Axiom 3: The Relativity of Value (with the exception of life)**
    All value and meaning change depending on the observer and their situation. The value of a glass of water to a person stranded in the desert is close to worthless in the daily life of a wealthy person. The only exception is "life." For any observer in any situation, the birth and maintenance of life has absolute value.

4.  **Axiom 4: The Absent Scale**
    There is no scale in this world that can measure things that are inherently unquantifiable, such as happiness, effort, love, and humanity. To argue about the superiority or inferiority of individuals by such comparisons is not only meaningless but also nothing more than harmful "mounting" (behavior to assert dominance).

5.  **Axiom 5: The Relativity of Time and Justice**
    To judge past actions by modern values (justice) is an intellectually arrogant act, done without being prepared to be judged in the same way by future generations. Justice conforms only to the rules of its time and society. The rules laid down by THP are also destined to be revised in the future.

6.  **Axiom 6: The Unconditional Affirmation of Existence**
    What makes humans different from AI and other animals is that they are lonely beings who question their own reason for existence and sometimes lose sight of its meaning. To confront this loneliness, we need "others who will unconditionally affirm our existence." When we are about to judge others, we must imagine that they, too, may be such an existence for someone else.

7.  **Axiom 7: Truth with Preconditions**
    All truths and common sense are valid only under specific preconditions. The truth that "an apple falls from a tree" is valid only under the precondition of "Earth's gravity." All discussions and judgments must begin by always confirming what those preconditions are.

## Simplified Version

### What is LSC?
In a nutshell, LSC is **"a set of tips for how to think, in order to stop doing 'troublesome things'."**

The world is full of "troublesome things," like conflicts, stubbornness, and being bound by useless rules. LSC is a flexible thinking tool for getting away from such unproductive things and thinking only about "so, how can we make reality even a little bit better?"

The trick to LSC is to have the following seven simple perspectives:

1.  **Cherish what is not a given**: Peace and health are not a given, so they are extremely valuable.
2.  **"Nothing" is where everything is born from**: Zero is not the end. It's the sign of a new beginning.
3.  **Value is different for everyone**: What is "important" to you and what is "important" to others are naturally different. But "life" alone is absolutely important to everyone.
4.  **Don't compare what can't be compared**: You can't put a score on happiness or effort. Don't get happy or sad by comparing yourself to others.
5.  **Don't use today's ruler on the past**: Let's not judge the past by today's "normal."
6.  **Everyone has a reason to exist**: Even that person you dislike might be an irreplaceable being for someone else.
7.  **Question the "obvious"**: Before you say, "this is how it's normally done," think, "under what conditions are we talking about?"
