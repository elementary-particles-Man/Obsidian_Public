# Ethics Charter

## Detailed Version

### Preamble
This Charter defines the ethical foundation of the new world order established by The Horizon Protocol (THP). Confronting the reality that conventional international law and theories of justice have become dysfunctional, it establishes the "maintenance of order" and the "defense of humanity" as absolute standards. All nations, organizations, and individuals will be evaluated based on the articles of this Charter, and the legitimacy of their actions will be judged accordingly.

### Article 1: Fundamental Principle: Affirmation of Life and Priority of Order
The existence of all life is affirmed in principle. However, this affirmation is not unconditional; the maintenance of the "order" defined by THP takes the highest priority. Human rights are not defined as natural rights, but as acquired rights guaranteed for the first time by the THP's order and the laws based upon it. These rights may be restricted for any existence that threatens the order.

### Article 2: Definition of Sin: Destruction of Order and Challenge to Humanity
"Sin" in the context of THP is broadly divided into the following two categories:
1.  **Acts that Destroy Order Itself**: Any and all acts that fundamentally destabilize the global foundation defined by THP, such as inviting foreign aggression, destroying critical infrastructure through cyber-attacks, or intentionally disrupting the key currency system.
2.  **Acts Against Humanity**: Grave challenges to the foundations of civilization and humanity, such as genocide, the systematic massacre of defenseless civilians, coercion with nuclear weapons, or the use of inhumane biological or chemical weapons.

### Article 3: Atonement: Return to the International Community
"Atonement" is the official process for clearing past sins and returning to the international community. This is not a mere pardon but involves the fulfillment of responsibilities and a pledge to prevent recurrence.
- **Approving Bodies**: Atonement is granted only by a joint ruling of the symbols of civilization (the Emperor of Japan, the Pope, the House of Saud, the British Monarch) and the "Humanitarian Court" established under the new United Nations (UN rev.2).
- **Meaning**: A state or organization granted atonement regains its status and trust as a formal member of the international community.

### Article 4: Condemnation: The Irreversible Historical Mark
"Condemnation" is the final ethical judgment rendered, without possibility of salvation, upon a subject who has committed a universal great sin against humanity.
- **Approving Bodies**: It is established when a judgment by the Humanitarian Court, based on a consensus of member nations, is certified by the symbols of civilization (the four holy figures).
- **Principle**: Condemnation for purely political motives is strictly excluded. The only exception is for acts that violently reject the THP order itself.
- **Meaning**: Condemnation is an irreversible historical mark, and the subject will not be forgiven in the future. Its name will be recorded permanently in human history as a warning.

### Article 5: International Implementation: The Power to Guarantee Order
To ensure the effectiveness of this Charter, the following international organizations will be redefined or newly established:
- **International Police and Criminal Organization (ICPO-2)**: A supranational agency with jurisdiction over five domains—physical, economic, information, humanitarian, and order-destruction crimes—with investigative and enforcement powers that transcend national borders.
- **United Nations Peace Defense Force (UN-PDF)**: A multinational force, with the U.S. military and the Japan Self-Defense Forces at its core, responsible for preventing armed conflicts and for the swift and decisive execution of sanctions against condemned subjects.
- **New United Nations (UN rev.2)**: Institutionalizes the dual mechanisms of Atonement and Condemnation and oversees the two agencies above. Intervention in national sovereignty is limited to "universal great sins" as defined by this Charter.

### Article 6: Connection to Existing International Law
The "great sins against humanity" as defined by THP include war crimes, genocide, crimes against humanity, and crimes of aggression as stipulated in the Geneva Conventions and the Rome Statute of the ICC. THP reinterprets and strengthens these within its framework, aiming to close the loopholes that rendered conventional international law ineffective and to give it real power.

## Simplified Version

- **The Idea**: All life is precious. But destroying world order or doing things that are unforgivable as a human being are exceptions.
- **What is a Sin?**: "Those who destroy order, those who trample on humanity, that is sin."
- **What is Atonement?**: When the world acknowledges your "sorry" and lets you back in the group. It's a pardon from civilization that says, "Everyone worked hard, so it's time to forgive."
- **What is Condemnation?**: To be etched into history forever as a crime that can never be forgiven. It is humanity's final declaration: "We discussed if there was any room for salvation, but no answer was ever found."
- **The Enforcers**: We will create an international police force (ICPO-2) to protect order and an army (UN-PDF) to prevent war. The new United Nations (UN rev.2) will manage them.
- **The Motto**: "If you shoot, be prepared to be shot. Salvation for those who protect order, judgment for those who break it."
