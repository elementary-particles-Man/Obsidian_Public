# Religion Charter

## Detailed Version

### Preamble: The Horizontality of Religion and the Personal Domain
The Horizon Protocol (THP) redefines religion as belonging to the domain of an individual's inner spirituality, completely separate from the state and politics. Historically, religion has often been tied to political power and used as a tool for conflict and oppression. To avoid repeating this error, THP guarantees the "horizontality" of all religions and thoughts. The foundation of coexistence is placed on the principle of mutual recognition: "Your religion is right. And my religion is also right."

### Article 1: Freedom of Belief and Separation of Religion and State
All individuals have the right to freely choose, explore, practice, or abandon their own beliefs. The state must not favor or suppress any particular religion and is obligated to maintain a thorough separation of religion and state in public education and administration. Belief is a matter of individual conscience, and state intervention is not permitted under any circumstances.

### Article 2: The Duty of Mutual Respect
All believers have a duty to respect the beliefs and thoughts of others and to acknowledge their value. The act of asserting the absolute nature of one's own doctrine and insulting or attacking the beliefs of others as "wrong" or "evil" is considered "intolerance," which is forbidden by THP, and an act that destroys the spiritual peace of the world.

### Article 3: Non-interference with Science and Medicine
Religion deals with the spiritual domain, while science deals with physical reality; they are different dimensions of knowledge. Especially in medicine, standard treatment based on scientific evidence is given the highest priority as the best means to protect life. Obstructing access to standard medical care for oneself or others (especially children) for religious reasons is a grave violation against life and will not be tolerated.

### Article 4: The Strict Definition and Exclusion of Cults
Freedom of religion and thought is guaranteed only to the extent that it does not infringe on the rights of others. Any organization that exhibits even one of the following characteristics is defined not as a religion to be protected, but as a "cult (violent apparatus)" to be excluded under the THP Ethics Charter:
1.  **Human Rights Violations**: Systematically infringes on the life, body, property, or dignity of its followers or third parties.
2.  **Intolerance Towards Others**: Justifies violence, discrimination, or exclusion against those with different religions or thoughts as part of its doctrine.
3.  **Anti-Scientific Stance**: Explicitly denies scientific knowledge or standard medical care, endangering the lives and health of its followers.
4.  **Forced Social Isolation**: Isolates followers from their families and society, forcing absolute dependence on the organization.
5.  **Deification of the Leader**: Demands absolute obedience and service to the private interests of a specific leader, rather than to the doctrine itself.

### Epilogue
Under this Charter, religion will reclaim its original role of guiding, comforting, and enriching the human spirit. Anything that harms others and brings chaos to society is violence in the name of faith, and it has no place in the world of THP.

## Simplified Version

- **All Religions are Equal**: "Your religion is right. My religion is also right." This is the basic rule.
- **Freedom to Believe or Not Believe**: What you believe or don't believe is completely your own choice. The state will not interfere.
- **Don't Make Fun of Other Religions**: You must not insult or attack what other people believe.
- **Respect Science and Medicine**: Prayer is important. But for treating illness and injury, scientific medical care is the top priority.
- **A "Cult" is Not a Religion**: A dangerous group that hurts people, isolates them from society, or denies science is not a religion, but just violence.
