# Ops KPI Dashboard

## Detailed Version (Dashboard for Operators)

### Preamble: Real-time Diagnosis of the World
This dashboard is the integrated measurement platform for The Horizon Protocol (THP) operations team to monitor and diagnose the stability of the post-Walpurgis global economy and social infrastructure in real time. Each KPI (Key Performance Indicator) covers financial markets, supply chains, geopolitical risks, and more, and each has a "critical trigger" set. The activation of a trigger serves as the basis for judging the need for automatic intervention by THP (such as activating a swap).

---

### I. Global Financial Markets
**Objective: To monitor the collapse risk of the old world financial system and the transition status to the new system.**
| Indicator | Current Value/Status | Critical Trigger | Importance/Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UST Bid-to-Cover Ratio** | Avg. 2.3x | <1.5x | The most important indicator of declining confidence in U.S. Treasuries. A dip below 1.5x raises concerns of a de facto default. |
| **TGA Balance** | ~$500B | >$850B | The U.S. Treasury's cash balance. An abnormally high level signifies liquidity absorption from the market, indicating financial tightening. |
| **DXY Index** | ~105 | <100 | An index showing the comprehensive value of the dollar. A fall below 100 suggests the end of its status as the key currency. |
| **Gold Price** | Rising | >$2,500/oz | Indicates an accelerating flight from the dollar and fiat currencies (a shift to hard assets). |

## Simplified Version (Summary for the Public)

### What is THP Monitoring?

The Horizon Protocol (THP) monitors the "health" of the world 24/7 to ensure that our peaceful lives are not threatened by global economic turmoil or the selfish actions of certain countries. The tool for this is the "Ops KPI Dashboard."

- **We are watching the flow of money.**
    - We constantly check if the financial system of the United States, the center of the world, is on the verge of collapse, or if the value of the dollar is plummeting. If there are dangerous signs, we respond immediately.

- **We are watching the problems of large countries.**
    - We carefully monitor China's real estate problems and Europe's economic instability to prevent them from causing a worldwide panic.

- **We are watching the systems that protect our lives.**
    - In a financial crisis, does the "swap" system, a mutual aid mechanism for countries to lend money to each other, activate within one hour?
    - If semiconductors become unavailable due to war or other reasons, can alternative production start immediately in other countries?
    - We use AI to detect if any country is spreading fake news.

This dashboard is, in a manner of speaking, **the world's health report card**. Based on this report card, THP acts swiftly and accurately before problems get bigger.
