## Plain Language Version (Summary for the Public)

The Horizon Protocol (THP) is a system that monitors the world's "health status" 24/7.

-   **Flow of Money**: We monitor U.S. Treasury auctions, the dollar index, and the price of gold.
-   **Major Risks**: We watch carefully to ensure that crises in China, Europe, and emerging markets do not trigger a chain reaction.
-   **Credit and Daily Life**: We track bankruptcies, banking instability, food, and energy using hard numbers.
-   **Safety Net**: We verify that our safety nets, like currency swaps and redundant semiconductor supplies, are functional.

This dashboard is the **world's health report card** and the foundation for an immediate response before problems grow larger.
