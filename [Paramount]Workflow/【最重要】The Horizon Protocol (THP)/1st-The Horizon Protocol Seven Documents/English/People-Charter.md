# People Charter

## Detailed Version

### Preamble: Redefining "People"
The history of the 20th century repeated the tragedy of peoples being linked to "nations" in the name of nationalism, justifying supremacy over others and igniting conflicts. The Horizon Protocol (THP) declares an end to this error and liberates peoples from the yoke of nations and territories. A "people" in this Charter is defined as a human group that shares a history, culture, and language, and serves as a source of an individual's identity and spiritual anchor. All peoples, regardless of their size or the length of their history, are to be respected as equal, horizontal existences.

### Article 1: Freedom of Ethnic Identity
All individuals have the right to freely choose the people with which they identify and to inherit its culture and pride. Ethnic identity is not to be enforced by birth or bloodline but is a part of the right to self-determination based on individual dignity. The state shall not infringe upon this freedom for any reason.

### Article 2: Protection of Culture and Tradition
The unique cultures, languages, and traditions nurtured by each people are the intangible assets of all humanity. THP will support the protection and succession of these assets if they are in danger of extinction. However, this does not apply if the tradition in question conflicts with the THP Ethics Charter (e.g., by violating human rights).

### Article 3: The Duty of Mutual Respect and Prohibition of Discrimination and Aggression
People belonging to different ethnic groups have a duty to respect each other's values and history. Any attempt to establish one's own ethnic identity through aggression, discrimination, or the distortion/denial of another people's history is considered a grave violation that destroys world order.

### Article 4: The Absolute Rejection of Supremacy
No people shall claim to be superior to or more special than any other. All forms of ethnic supremacism, whether based on historical circumstances, economic power, or cultural influence, are the root of the tragedies of the 20th century and will not be tolerated in the world of THP. There is no hierarchy among peoples; there is only horizontal diversity.

### Article 5: The Principle of Coexistence and the Degeneration into Violence
A people functions as a positive community that saves individuals from loneliness and fosters a rich culture. However, when its energy is directed toward hatred of another people or the plunder of resources, it is no longer considered a people but is judged to have degenerated into a "violent apparatus" to be condemned under the THP Ethics Charter.

### Epilogue
This Charter is a guidepost for transforming peoples from tools of conflict into a foundation that celebrates cultural diversity and supports the coexistence of humanity.

## Simplified Version

- **All Peoples are Free and Equal**: There is no high or low among peoples; everyone is equal. You can decide your own ethnic identity.
- **Culture is Everyone's Treasure**: The cultures and languages of all the different peoples are a treasure for all of humanity and should be cherished.
- **Respect Each Other**: It is absolutely forbidden to make fun of, attack, or discriminate against other peoples.
- **"We are Number One" is Forbidden**: Thinking that "our people are special" is the source of conflict and is forbidden.
- **No Violence**: You must not use your people as a tool to hurt others.
