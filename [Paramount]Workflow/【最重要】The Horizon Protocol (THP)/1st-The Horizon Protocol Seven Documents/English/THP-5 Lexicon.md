# THP-5 Lexicon

## Introduction

The Horizon Protocol (THP) is a comprehensive framework aimed at the survival and reconstruction of civilization in a post-Walpurgis world, where existing political, economic, and social systems have become dysfunctional. This lexicon aims to serve as a guide to understanding the unique concepts that constitute THP and the philosophy behind them.

This document is structured in two layers: a **"Research Version"** that explains the ideological foundations of THP, and an **"Article Version"** that describes the essence in simpler terms.

## A. Core THP Terminology

### The Horizon Protocol (THP)

- **Research Version:** A comprehensive crisis response and civilization reconstruction protocol aimed at maintaining the minimum functions of human civilization and achieving a soft landing into a new framework for international cooperation (UN rev.2) in a world where the existing international order has collapsed due to Walpurgis. Its core consists of three pillars: stabilization of the financial system (Fiat-Rev2), securing physical survival infrastructure (Lifeline Plan), and new ethical norms (E-MAD). THP is not a static plan but a dynamic system that constantly interacts with reality and self-corrects based on LSC-OS.

- **Article Version:** A comprehensive plan to protect our lives and create new world rules after a global catastrophe (Walpurgis). It is a compass for the future that proposes stabilizing financial flows, securing essential goods like water, food, and electricity, and advocating new ethics to prevent conflicts.

### Walpurgis

- **Research Version:** A collective term for the global systemic financial crisis triggered by the failure of the US Treasury auction on September 30, 2025, and the accompanying geopolitical, social, and humanitarian complex disasters. This is not a cyclical economic crisis but an Extinction-Level Event, the collapse of order itself, caused by the structural flaws of the global financial system (especially the procyclicality of SLR and CCP margin systems) built after the 2008 financial crisis, resonating with geopolitical tensions and societal divisions.

- **Article Version:** An unavoidable global catastrophe caused by a "misconfiguration" of the world's financial system. It's a "storm of disaster" where not only the economy but also international conflicts and social unrest erupt simultaneously.

### THP-DEATH (Terminal State)

- **Research Version:** A state where the exchangeability of rewards is lost due to currency collapse, Grid Management (GM) ceases, and power generation, transmission, markets, and clearing irreversibly drop out in a chain reaction. While L1-L3 are "controllable regions," DEATH means the control mechanisms (humans and institutions) themselves cease, so there are no operators to execute a recovery process. The only path is to transition to a **separate new system = THP (Post-Order)**.

- **Article Version:** A state where money becomes worthless, salaries lose meaning, and people who operate the power grid can no longer work. At that moment, electricity → markets → currency stop all at once, and there's no one or no system left to restore them.

### Lifeline Resource Plan

- **Research Version:** A practical plan to maintain minimum international logistics to prevent humanitarian crises even after the collapse of trade finance due to Walpurgis. Based on a minilateral agreement centered on Japan, India, Singapore, and Malaysia (JIMS), it combines non-dollar alternative settlement rails with government-guaranteed trade finance (L/C guarantees, reinsurance pools). Target items are limited to lifeline resources related to water, medicine, electricity, and food.

- **Article Version:** A plan to ensure that even if the world falls into chaos, essential things for our survival like water, medicine, electricity, and food do not stop. It's a lifeline that ensures safe transport routes and new payment methods through cooperation among trusted nations.
