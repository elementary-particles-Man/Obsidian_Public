# World Redefinition Charter

## Detailed Version

### Preamble: The Death Certificate of the Old World
The "national stories" and "ethnic myths" forged in the 20th century have begun to self-destruct under the weight of their own internal contradictions and historical deceptions. Japan's "international contributions," Great Britain's "faded glory," Italy's "historical reconciliation," Israel's "promised land"—all of these have deepened today's severe global conflicts and dysfunctions by relying on self-serving historical interpretations and avoiding a direct confrontation with their difficult pasts.

In contrast, Germany has presented a "narrative of responsibility." By not averting their eyes from the worst parts of their past, integrating it into their national identity, and shouldering it as a permanent responsibility, they were able to build a foundation of trust in post-war Europe. The truth this comparison reveals is singular: "A narrative that avoids a difficult past is destined to fail."

In the post-Walpurgis world, myths and selective interpretations of history no longer have the power to bind the world order. The sole foundation of the new order is the universal and transparent rule set forth in the THP Ethics Charter: "to punish those who destroy order and those who trample on humanity." This declaration serves as both a death certificate for the narratives of the old world and a birth certificate for the new order led by THP.

### Article 1: The Priority of Universal Order
The one and only absolute justice in the new world is the "maintenance of order." No act that destroys the order itself or violates humanity can be justified, regardless of the name it borrows—be it ethnicity, religion, nation, or ideology. The legitimate members of the world are all subjects, regardless of the size of their nation or their economic power, who respect this universal order and bear the responsibility of maintaining it.

### Article 2: The Integration of Responsibility and Atonement
Past sins cannot be resolved through oblivion or relativization. They must be integrated permanently into the identity of a nation or people as "responsibility." As the example of Germany shows, it is the succession of responsibility that builds trust in the future. THP establishes a gateway for "Atonement," which allows for a return to the international community, on the premise that this responsibility is fulfilled.

### Article 3: The Irreversibility of Condemnation
Acts defined as "universal great sins" in the THP Ethics Charter—such as war crimes, genocide, and crimes against humanity—are to be "condemned" by an ethical judgment of all humanity that transcends political decisions. This condemnation is an irreversible historical mark that cannot be overturned by any change in era or political situation. This is humanity's pledge to the future.

### Article 4: The Horizontality of Peoples and Religions
All peoples and all religions are equal and horizontal in their culture, history, and values; no hierarchy exists. Their diversity is to be respected and protected as an asset to all humanity. However, when a group denies others, claims its own supremacy, or justifies violence, it is no longer considered a people or a religion, but a "cult" or a "violent apparatus" to be condemned by THP. The foundation of coexistence lies in the mutual recognition that "your truth and my truth are both true."

### Article 5: Scientific Realism
Science is not omnipotent, but as "practical knowledge" based on objective data and verifiable processes, it is to be the basis for all decision-making. In medicine especially, standard treatment based on scientific evidence is prioritized as the best means to protect life. Obstructing access to standard medical care for religious or cultural reasons is considered a grave violation against life.

### Article 6: The Co-Creation of the Future Narrative
The narrative of "justice," once monopolized by nations, peoples, and religions in the old world, has lost its power. The only narrative that can weave the future in the post-Walpurgis world is the process of all subjects co-creating a common order under the three pillars raised by THP: "Transparency, Fairness, and Accuracy."

### Epilogue: An Evolving Protocol
The seven documents of THP, including this declaration, are not a completed scripture. They are an evolving protocol that must be constantly rewritten by future generations through democratic processes to fit the reality of their time. Our goal is not the realization of a perfect world, but the perpetual, unending effort to pursue a world where all life can maintain its dignity and enjoy peace in any era.

## Simplified Version

- **The End of Old Stories**: Self-serving historical interpretations and myths are no longer valid. Countries that ignore their past will fail.
- **The New Rule**: There is only one rule for the world: "Punish those who destroy order and those who do unforgivable things to people."
- **Sin and Responsibility**: Don't forget past sins; accept them as a continuing responsibility. That's how you build trust for the future.
- **Peoples and Religions**: All peoples and religions are equal. No one is better than anyone else. The basic idea is, "Yours is right, and mine is right, too."
- **Science is Important**: Trust data and facts over beliefs. Especially in medicine, scientific standard treatment comes first.
- **How to Build the Future**: We will create a new story together under the rules of "Transparency, Fairness, and Accuracy."
- **These Rules Aren't Perfect Either**: It's okay for future generations to change this declaration. The important thing is to keep striving for a peaceful world.
