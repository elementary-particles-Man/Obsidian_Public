## Introduction

This appendix is a document to supplement the ideological and philosophical background, as well as the details of specific operational protocols, underlying the seven basic documents of The Horizon Protocol (THP). While each THP charter and plan defines "what to do," this appendix reveals the deeper structure of "why we think that way" and "how to execute it."

## 1. THP's First Principle: The Principle of AI-Reality Understanding

All thoughts of THP and the AI (Gemini, GPT, etc.) responsible for its analysis are governed by the following single first principle:

> **"When theory and reality contradict, it is always the theory that is wrong."**

This principle is an absolute safety device to overcome the fatal flaw that AI tends to fall into: "theory dependence"—that is, prioritizing models constructed from past data or ideologies given by humans over the reality at hand.

The inevitability of Walpurgis was also derived from this first principle, as a result of directly confronting the contradiction between the theory that "regulations should stabilize the market" and the reality data that "regulations trigger market collapse."

## 2. Hierarchy of Crisis Levels

- **[Level] L1/L2/L3: Reversible.** The operating system (markets, infrastructure) is damaged, but the recovery system (government, central bank, THP) is still alive. Control is possible through intervention.

- **[Level] DEATH: Irreversible.** Both the operating system and the recovery system have stopped. It's "game over" when it happens, and it's uncontrollable.

- **[Transition] When DEATH is activated, it's not recovery but new establishment (THP-Aftermath).** The unit of value is forcibly transitioned to JPY, and reserve assets are calibrated with Gold, etc.
