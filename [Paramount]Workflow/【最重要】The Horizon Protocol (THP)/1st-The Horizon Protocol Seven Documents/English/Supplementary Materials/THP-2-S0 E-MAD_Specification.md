# THP-6 Appendix - Supplementary Materials

### **Ethics Charter Supplementary Material: E-MAD Official Specification (v1.2 Operational Version)**

#### **0. Preamble and General Principles**

**Preamble**
This document establishes the official specifications for **E-MAD (Effective Multilateral Assured Denial)** as a core operational subsystem of **The Horizon Protocol (THP)**. While the **Ethics Charter** provides an abstract and normative foundation (human morality, atonement, condemnation), this specification prioritizes **concreteness and rigor**. E-MAD defines how it is measured, executed, and governed, ensuring compatibility with each THP charter and operational dashboard.

E-MAD is a **non-violent, transparent, and multilateral** framework. Its purpose is not retaliation, but to **surely deny the success of aggressors**, thereby preventing wars and catastrophic escalations.

#### **1. Conceptual Basis**
1.  **Boundary as Human Morality**: The boundary between *atonable* sins and sins that *deserve condemnation*.
2.  **Atonement** — *Red*: Acts of aggression. The entity can reintegrate into the international community after atonement and fulfillment of obligations. **Function**: Restorative and reintegrative.
3.  **Condemnation** — *Crimson*: Nuclear attacks or crimes against humanity. Non-violent and absolute measures. **No forgiveness**, and the stigma is permanent. **Function**: Protective and exclusive.
4.  **Principle of Success Denial**: To make the act of aggression itself **irrational**.

#### **2. E-MAD Hierarchical Response System**
**5-stage** of scale.

-   **Blue (Baseline)**: Normal state. Continuous monitoring and civic education.
-   **Yellow (Alert)**: Early warning. Diplomatic engagement.
-   **Orange (Critical Point)**: Critical state. Multilateral diplomatic intervention and pressure through non-punitive **price signals** (risk premium surcharge).
-   **Red (Atonement)**: Aggressive act confirmed. Activates **multi-faceted success denial measures** such as reparations, risk premiums, access restrictions, compliance investments, and monitoring obligations. A path to social reintegration is indicated based on the **Atonement Difficulty Index (ADI)**.
-   **Crimson (Condemnation)**: Nuclear attack or crimes against humanity. Activates absolute and non-violent measures such as **termination of all debt privileges** (**one strike out**). No appeal is allowed for the perpetrator.

## Application to Ethnic Conflicts (Phased Sanctions)

- Stage 1: Increase in funding, settlement, and insurance rates (targets the violating entity).
- Stage 2: Cut off essential material pipelines (excluding medical and food supplies).
- Stage 3: Permanent establishment of corridor security by UN-PDF.
- Trigger conditions are linked to People-Charter KPIs, and the final judgment is in the name of the Council of Human Morality.

#### **3. Measurement Indicators**
1.  **E-MAD Score (0–100)**: A composite indicator aggregating denial capabilities across multiple domains.
2.  **Estimated Loss**: Quantifies the financial loss of aggressors, victims, and third parties.
3.  **Atonement Difficulty Index (ADI, 0–100)**: A multi-faceted indicator measuring the difficulty of social reintegration in Red cases.
4.  **Transparency Requirements**: All data, models, and code are open-sourced, and independent audits are mandatory.

#### **4. Governance and Process**

1.  **Tripartite Governance**:
    -   **Expert Committee**: Methodology, models, quality control.
    -   **Citizen Assembly**: Ensures legitimacy through deliberation.
    -   **Council of Human Morality**: The **sole arbitrating body** for E-MAD stage determination.

2.  **Four Sages — Advisory Body**:
    -   **No voting rights. Not a member of the Council of Human Morality**.
    -   **Crimson cases**: Can exercise a one-time **right to request reconsideration**.
    -   **Red cases**: Can exercise a one-time **right to request amnesty** after substantial progress in ADI.
    -   All opinions are published as public memoranda, and the final decision is with the Council of Human Morality.

3.  **Due Process, Evidence Standards, Appeals**:
    -   **Evidence Standards**: Red = **high probability**, Crimson = **beyond reasonable doubt**.
    -   **Appeals**: No appeal for perpetrators in Crimson cases. Possible in Red cases based on ADI goals.
    -   **Humanitarian Exception (Zero-Stop Principle)**: Medical supplies, food, water, and emergency communications are never stopped.

### **Appendices**

#### **Annex A — Color Classification Thresholds**
E-MAD Score Range (initial proposal. Updated quarterly via public RFC)
-   **Blue**: ≥ 80
-   **Yellow**: 65–79
-   **Orange**: 50–64
-   **Red**: < 50 (however, immediate transition to Red upon "execution of aggression/attack" trigger)
-   **Crimson**: Immediately applied regardless of score upon activation of human morality condemnation trigger (nuclear/genocide, etc.)

#### **Annex B — ADI (Atonement Difficulty Index) Initial Weights**
Vector and Weights (Total = 1.00)
-   a. Reparation Capital: 0.30
-   b. Risk Premium Coefficient × Duration: 0.20
-   c. Access Restriction Period: 0.15
-   d. Compliance Verification: 0.15
-   e. Victim Relief: 0.15
-   f. Minimization of Humanitarian Impact: 0.05
*Aggregation: Weighted geometric mean of normalized indicators → ADI. Reintegration review is possible at 60 or higher.*

#### **Annex C — Risk Premium Assignment Formula for Orange**
Proposed Coefficient: `RP = β0 + β1·ESG_risk + β2·SupplyFrag + β3·SanctionProb`
*Updated weekly. β coefficients determined by public RFC, and regression equation and dataset are open-sourced.*

#### **Annex D — Standard Contract Clauses (Model Clauses)**
-   **[Crimson Acceleration Clause / Loss of Benefit of Term Clause upon Crimsonization]**
    "E-MAD protocolに基づくCrimson判定が下された場合、人道的ゼロストップ例外を除き、全ての債務特権は終了し、全ての未払い債務は即時に弁済期を迎えるものとする。当事者は、参加する全ての法域においてこの決定が相互承認されることを確認する。"
-   **[Red Rehabilitation Clause / Rehabilitation Clause upon Redization]**
    "Red事案は、独立した監視の下でADI閾値が満たされるまで、リスクプレミアムの上乗せ、時限的なアクセス制限、および第三者預託によるコンプライアンス投資の対象となる。"

#### **Annex E — 適正手続き様式（雛形）**
-   判定通知書（Orange/Red/Crimson）: 事実要旨／証拠要約／適用条項／救済手段
-   証拠書類一式: データ出典・時刻印・検算コード
-   上訴フォーム（Redのみ）: 不服理由／追加証拠／是正提案
-   再審議覚書（四聖）: 一度限り／公開意見書

#### **Annex F — 四聖の権限プロトコル（峻別）**
-   議決権なし。人倫評議会と制度上分離。
-   Crimson: 再審議要請権（一度限り、提出→公開→評議会審理）。
-   Red: 特赦要請権（ADI進捗到達後、一度限り）。
-   全て公開メモランダムとしてアーカイブされる。

#### **Annex G — 人道的ゼロストップ（人道例外の運用）**
-   **範囲**: 医療、食料、水、救援通信、ワクチン／必須医薬品の物流。
-   **実装**: 常設のホワイトリストと第三者監査。停止イベントゼロを設計目標（KPI）とする。

- 四聖は諮問（無投票）。本仕様は THP-ORF／Ethics Charter と一致させる。
