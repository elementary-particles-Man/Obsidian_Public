# THP-7-S0 Aftermath Protocol v1.0

## 1. Activation Conditions

This protocol is activated immediately and automatically upon the declaration of **Gate-D (DEATH / TERMINAL)** in the `THP-7 Ops-KPI Dashboard`. This signifies that "recovery" of existing systems is impossible, and all resources are to be shifted to "new system establishment."

## 2. Operational Package

### A) Emergency JPY Payroll Facility (E-JPF)

- **Purpose:** To guarantee salary payments for personnel essential to maintaining civilization's critical infrastructure, such as electricity, water, medical services, communications, and food supply, thereby preventing the complete shutdown of Grid Management (GM).

- **Design:**

    1. **Unit of Claim:** All claims shall be denominated in **Japanese Yen (JPY)** as the numeraire. For value stabilization, Gold shall be the anchor asset, with a disciplinary band set (e.g., 20–30% Gold / 70–80% JPY).

    2. **Payment Methods:**

        - **JPY Vouchers:** Restricted-use vouchers usable only for purchasing lifeline goods (food, fuel, etc.).

        - **Mobile QR Payment:** QR code payment system usable even offline.

        - **Paper Vouchers:** Backup in case of digital infrastructure failure.

    3. **Settlement:** Payments to material suppliers shall be settled based on the **JPY-Parities** described below. If the banking system fails, intergovernmental settlement through the JIMS corridor (Japan–India–Malaysia–Singapore) will guarantee payments.

    4. **Control and Audit:** Usage is strictly limited by MCC (Merchant Category Code) control. Identity verification adheres to WAU/KAIRO-P protocols, and all transactions are auditable on a public ledger (Witness-Only).

- **Duration:** This shall be a provisional measure for **T+90 days** after activation. Extension is subject to the judgment of THP Command.

### B) Publication of JPY-Parities (Provisional Fix)

- **Purpose:** To re-establish an anchor for the international pricing system amidst the collapse of the US dollar as a numeraire, preventing a regression to bartering. This is not about price control but providing **coordinates** for accounting and settlement.

- **Content:** Provisional fixed prices (Parity) in Japanese Yen for the following key goods and assets will be published:

    - **JG-Par:** JPY/Gold

    - **JS-Par:** JPY/Silver

    - **JOIL-Par:** JPY/WTI (Crude Oil)

    - **JGR-Par:** JPY/Grain (Basket of major grains)

    - **JWA-Par:** JPY/Water (Based on desalination and transport costs)

### C) L/C Government Guarantee + Reinsurance Pool

- **Purpose:** To unfreeze international trade finance and resume international transport of lifeline resources.

- **Operation:**

    1. **Scope:** Limited to items related to the four sectors: water, medical, electricity, and food.

    2. **Guarantee:** The Japanese government will 100% guarantee Letters of Credit (L/C) by maximizing the use of existing legal authorities of MOF/JBIC/JICA.

    3. **Reinsurance:** A reinsurance pool covering war and political upheaval risks will be established, starting from Japan and in cooperation with India and Singapore.

    4. **Settlement:** All settlements will be in Yen, and net settlement will be conducted intergovernmentally through the JIMS corridor. Contract clauses will explicitly prohibit **pseudo-dollarization (immediate exchange to dollars after transaction)**.

### D) Grid Blackstart Shield

- **Purpose:** To prevent the **collapse of the "recovery system" itself** involved in the restoration and maintenance of the grid (power network).

- **Operation:**

    1. **Protection of Minimum Personnel:** Minimum personnel required for power system maintenance and restart will be listed as "black box" personnel, and their salary payments will be guaranteed with the highest priority by E-JPF.

    2. **Securing Fuel Corridors:** Fuel transport routes to power plants will be designated as "humanitarian corridors," ensuring escort by UN-PDF and highest priority in customs clearance processes.

    3. **Communication Redundancy:** Redundancy of communication means such as radio, satellite, and emergency power will be ensured to maintain command and control.

## 3. Narrative and Compliance

### A) Basic Message (Internal and External)

1. **"Yen is the unit of value, lifeline corridors are open"** — Building international trust through auditable transparency, not aiming for new hegemony.

2. **"Supporting cities, not regimes"** — Aid is limited to infrastructure maintenance personnel, and restricted-use vouchers prevent diversion to military or security agencies.

3. **"Time-limited, quantity-limited, log-public"** — All interventions are provisional, and public audits (Witness-Only) prevent politicization.

### B) Prohibition/Deterrence Clauses (Contractual Compliance)

- **Shadow-USD Clause:** Prohibits immediate dollar exchange or dollar-denominated hedging of Yen-denominated settlements by contract. If a violation is found, full clawback of guaranteed funds will be exercised.

- **Non-Military Use Clause:** Requires an oath that supplied materials will be used only for civilian purposes and consent to unannounced on-site audits.

- **Public Audit Clause:** All transaction logs, with personal information hashed, will be made public in principle.

## 4. Timeline (T-0 ~ T+90)

- **T-0~+72h:** Gate-D judgment. Provisional Fix of JPY-Parities announced. E-JPF initiated for the top 5 sectors (power, water, medical, communications, food).

- **T+7d:** L/C Government Guarantee + Reinsurance Pool fully operational. Escort of fuel corridors begins.

- **T+14d:** EUR-R⇄JPY Snaplock introduced. Settlement fluctuations with remaining Eurozone functions will be suppressed by setting time windows.

- **T+30~90d:** Clearing bridges with ASEAN, India (INR), and Brazil (BRL) gradually connected. Gold-JPY disciplinary band (equivalent to 20-30% reserve ratio) officially announced.
