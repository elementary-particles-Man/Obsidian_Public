# Document No: SC-UN-DIPLO-001

**To:** The President of the United Nations Security Council, The Secretary-General of the United Nations **From:** The Permanent Mission of Japan to the United Nations (projected) **Subject:** Statement by the Government of Japan on the Armed Conflict on the Border of Thailand and Cambodia and the Threat to International Peace and Security

The Government of Japan expresses its deepest concern over the ongoing armed conflict in the border region of the Kingdom of Thailand and the Kingdom of Cambodia, and the grave humanitarian situation that has resulted.

**1. A Clear Challenge to International Law and the Charter of the United Nations** In the course of this series of military actions, there have been reports of the use of cluster munitions, an indiscriminate weapon, and deliberate attacks on civilian facilities, including schools. These acts are grave breaches of international humanitarian law and can under no circumstances be tolerated. Furthermore, the decision to resort to force, in disregard of the ruling of the International Court of Justice (ICJ), is a challenge to the very principles of the Charter of the United Nations, which obligates Member States to settle their disputes by peaceful means.

**2. A Compound Threat to Regional Stability** This matter has escalated beyond the scope of a mere bilateral territorial dispute. The stance of one party to persistently reject international mediation while continuing to make irrational claims backed by military superiority significantly undermines the solidarity of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) and invites instability throughout the entire region. This instability poses a direct threat to international peace and prosperity through the disruption of global supply chains and the risk of suspension of major regional agricultural production and export functions, thereby endangering food security.

**3. Fundamental Concerns Regarding State Sovereignty and the Capacity to Govern** The series of observed actions cannot be considered the result of rational policy decisions made by a sovereign state based on its national interest. We are deeply concerned by the possibility that the state governance of the Kingdom of Thailand is facing serious structural problems, deviating from normal civilian control and compelling decisions that diverge from international norms. Indications that the state's capacity to fulfill its basic responsibilities to its people and the international community has been significantly impaired cannot be overlooked.

**4. Urgent Appeal to the United Nations Security Council** The Government of Japan urgently calls upon the Security Council, which bears the primary responsibility for the maintenance of international peace and security, to take the following actions:

- To take up this matter as a formal agenda item of the Security Council and to adopt a strong presidential statement or resolution demanding an immediate and unconditional ceasefire.
    
- To request the Secretary-General to dispatch a fact-finding mission to ascertain the objective facts on the ground.
    
- To initiate a United-Nations-led mediation process as swiftly as possible, in close coordination with regional frameworks including ASEAN.
    

**5. Japan's Contribution** The Government of Japan stands ready to fully support all efforts by the international community toward a peaceful resolution of this issue. We are resolved to make the utmost contribution to the restoration of regional peace and stability through our active participation in UN-led diplomatic efforts and by providing humanitarian assistance to the affected civilian populations.
