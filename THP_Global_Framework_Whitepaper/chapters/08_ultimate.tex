\section{Ultimate: Theoretical Post-Fiat Coherence}
\subsection{Concept}
Ultimate is a post-fiat coherence regime in which value is anchored to structural consistency rather than credit. Money becomes an attestation of non-contradiction, and exchange synchronizes local truth states. This appendix is conceptual and not intended for policy adoption.

\subsection{Preconditions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item ITIF operating for multiple decades with global T-value stabilization.
  \item Universal CLEAR timestamp infrastructure under national control.
  \item Decline of opacity-driven incentives and reduction of credit dependence.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Formal Sketch}
\begin{align*}
  V &= \frac{C}{I + E}\\
  &\text{where } C=\text{structural coherence},\ I=\text{institutional inconsistency},\ E=\text{information entropy}
\end{align*}
In Ultimate, money corresponds to \(\Delta C\); credit and interest structures recede.

\subsection{Transition Path}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item ITIF consolidation: opacity cycles reduced, T-value institutionalized.
  \item CLEAR universalization: timestamp economy supplements credit functions.
  \item Decline of credit: limited to cashflow smoothing.
  \item Emergence: coherence-signaling replaces credit signaling; redistribution politics diminishes.
\end{enumerate}
