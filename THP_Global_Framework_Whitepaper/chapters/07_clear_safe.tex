\section{CLEAR: Non-Intrusive Verification Layer}
\subsection{Principles}
CLEAR is positioned as a verification utility: it does not store personal data, does not perform inference, fully preserves national sovereignty, and is operated exclusively by national authorities. Its remit is limited to timestamp and integrity proofs.

\subsection{Authority Model}
\begin{itemize}
  \item National nodes operated by sovereign institutions; regional nodes for redundancy; audit nodes for integrity checks.
  \item Forbidden capabilities: tracking, behavioral modeling, social scoring, or any centralized control functions.
  \item Allowed capabilities: hash consistency validation, timestamp reconciliation, key lifecycle attestation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Transparency Sync Protocol}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Quarterly transmission of institutional and structural indicators (budgets, timetables, audits, investment cycle lags).
  \item No access to national datasets; acts solely as an integrity oracle.
  \item Neutral mechanism for reporting transparency indicators across jurisdictions.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Interface with ITIF}
CLEAR underpins T-value verification without executing policy decisions. All fiscal and monetary authority remains domestic.
