# Note Verbale

**Ref:** NV/UN/2025/001

The Permanent Mission of Japan to the United Nations presents its compliments to the Secretariat of the United Nations and has the honour to transmit herewith the Statement by the Government of Japan regarding the armed conflict on the border of the Kingdom of Thailand and the Kingdom of Cambodia and the threat it poses to international peace and security.

The Government of Japan expresses its deepest concern over this grave situation, which challenges the fundamental principles of the Charter of the United Nations and risks severe destabilization of the region.

The Government of Japan requests that the attached Statement be circulated as a document of the Security Council.

The Permanent Mission of Japan to the United Nations avails itself of this opportunity to renew to the Secretariat of the United Nations the assurances of its highest consideration.

**[Date]** New York

**To:** The Secretariat of the United Nations New York
