**1. Executive Summary**

The European Union, in its current structural state, faces an imminent collapse in monetary coherence, industrial continuity, and defense coordination. The recent NATO Hague Summit confirmed that both economic and military sovereignty of EU states—particularly Germany—has been formally subordinated to U.S. fiscal and security mandates. Given the U.S. trajectory under the Trump administration, this subordination is likely to intensify.

In this context, we submit for emergency deliberation the implementation of **Fiat Rev.2**—a framework for expanding the operative functionality of the euro within the bounds of existing law. This framework offers an immediate and legally feasible pathway to restore economic autonomy, maintain intra-EU cohesion, and elevate France as the central stabilizing actor.

---

**2. Problem Statement**

- **Germany**: Functionally incapacitated. Industrial exports blocked by U.S. and Asian tariff regimes; energy dependence unresolved; military reconstitution infeasible within winter timeframe.
    
- **EU Institutions**: ECB and Commission lack operative integration between monetary issuance and fiscal execution. This disconnect renders them unable to apply fiat principles beyond accounting formalities.
    
- **France**: The only EU power with surviving energetic, nuclear, and military sovereignty. Yet bearing the full stabilizing burden without structural reform is unsustainable.
    
- **NATO**: Converted into a U.S.-dominated fiscal tribute system. Defense policy autonomy has been nullified.
    

---

**3. Fiat Rev.2 Framework Overview**

Fiat Rev.2 is **not MMT**. It is not theoretical, nor inflationary populism. It is a systems engineering solution to a collapsing fiscal-monetary interface:

- **Objective**: To allow euro-denominated fiscal operation within member states while preserving euro cohesion and ECB credit authority.
    
- **Mechanism**:
    
    - Creation of "national euro operating zones" pegged within a fixed euro exchange band (e.g., French Euro Zone, German Pegged Euro).
        
    - ECB authorizes special-purpose accounts for domestic infrastructure, energy, and defense payments.
        
    - These accounts function under conditional transparency and ECB oversight, but operate with fiat-style immediate disbursement authority.
        
- **Benefit**: Avoids treaty violations while restoring policy flexibility in critical domains.
    

---

**4. Structural Outcome**

Implementing Fiat Rev.2 will:

- Allow France to consolidate economic leadership without explicit dominance, avoiding political backlash.
    
- Create a functional monetary mechanism for intra-EU stabilization and external strategic autonomy.
    
- Prevent systemic collapse in Germany, Italy, Spain without requiring complete fiscal union.
    
- Transition the EU toward a sovereign monetary block capable of interfacing with a hostile multipolar world (U.S. tariffs, Chinese decoupling, Russian attrition).
